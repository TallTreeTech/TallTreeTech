Leave Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2025

Policy Statement: TallTree Technologies is committed to supporting employees' work-life balance and wellbeing. This policy outlines the various types of leave available to employees and the procedures for requesting and managing absence from work.

1. Scope

This policy applies to all employees of TallTree Technologies. Specific entitlements may vary based on employment status, length of service, and contractual terms.

2. Annual Leave

2.1 Entitlement

All full-time employees are entitled to a minimum of 28 days (5.6 weeks) of paid annual leave per year. This includes UK public holidays (bank holidays).

  • Full-time employees: 28 days (including 8 bank holidays) = 20 days annual leave + 8 bank holidays
  • Part-time employees: Pro-rata entitlement based on working hours
  • Enhanced entitlement: Employees with 5+ years' service may receive additional leave days as specified in their contract

2.2 Leave Year

The standard leave year runs from 1st January to 31st December. For employees joining part-way through the year, entitlement is calculated on a pro-rata basis.

2.3 Accrual for New Starters

  • Leave accrues from the first day of employment
  • During the first year: accrues at 2.33 days per month (for 28-day entitlement)
  • Employees may take leave in advance by agreement with their line manager
  • If employment ends and leave taken exceeds accrued entitlement, the excess will be deducted from final pay

3. Booking and Approval Process

3.1 Requesting Leave

All annual leave must be requested and approved in advance through the company's leave management system or by email to your line manager.

3.2 Notice Periods

  • Standard leave: At least 2 weeks' notice for leave of up to 5 days
  • Extended leave: At least 4 weeks' notice for leave of 6 days or more
  • School holidays: At least 6 weeks' notice for leave during popular periods
  • Emergency/short notice: Requests with less notice may be approved at manager's discretion

3.3 Approval

  • Line managers will respond to leave requests within 3 working days where possible
  • Leave is not confirmed until written approval is received
  • Employees should not make non-refundable bookings before leave is approved
  • The company reserves the right to refuse leave requests based on business needs

3.4 Considerations for Approval

When reviewing leave requests, managers will consider:

  • Business operational requirements
  • Project deadlines and commitments
  • Team coverage and other team members' leave
  • Fairness and consistency across the team
  • Whether adequate notice was provided

3.5 Cancelling Leave

  • Employees may cancel approved leave by notifying their manager as soon as possible
  • The company may require an employee to cancel leave in exceptional business circumstances, with as much notice as possible
  • Where the company cancels approved leave, reasonable expenses incurred will be reimbursed

4. Carry-Over Rules

4.1 Standard Carry-Over

  • Employees may carry over up to 5 days of unused annual leave into the next leave year
  • Carried-over leave must be taken by 31st March of the new leave year
  • Carried-over leave that is not taken by this date will be forfeited
  • Any carry-over must be agreed with your line manager

4.2 Exceptional Circumstances

Where an employee has been unable to take leave due to:

  • Long-term sickness absence
  • Maternity, paternity, or other family leave
  • Exceptional business reasons beyond their control

Additional carry-over may be permitted with HR approval, subject to legal requirements.

4.3 Encouraging Leave Usage

  • Employees are encouraged to take their full leave entitlement each year
  • Managers will monitor leave balances and discuss plans with employees who have significant unused leave
  • Taking regular breaks is important for wellbeing and performance

5. Sickness Absence

5.1 Notification Procedure

If you are unable to work due to illness or injury, you must:

  • Notify your line manager by phone or Slack before your normal start time on the first day of absence (or as soon as reasonably practicable)
  • Provide an indication of the expected duration of absence
  • Explain the nature of your illness (general terms are acceptable for privacy)
  • Provide daily updates for the first 3 days unless otherwise agreed
  • Keep in touch regarding your recovery and expected return date

5.2 Medical Certification

  • Days 1-7: Self-certification is sufficient
  • Day 8 onwards: A fit note (Statement of Fitness for Work) from a doctor is required
  • Fit notes must be submitted to HR and cover the entire period of absence
  • Additional fit notes must be provided if absence continues beyond the initial certificate

5.3 Return to Work

  • Notify your manager when you are ready to return to work
  • A return-to-work discussion will be held with your line manager
  • This is a supportive conversation to ensure you are fit to return and discuss any support needed
  • Reasonable adjustments or phased returns may be arranged where appropriate

5.4 Frequent or Long-Term Absence

If you have:

  • More than 3 separate periods of absence in a rolling 6-month period, or
  • More than 10 days of absence in a rolling 12-month period, or
  • A pattern of absence (e.g., around weekends, after leave)

A formal review meeting will be arranged to discuss your absence, identify any underlying health issues, and consider what support can be provided. This is not disciplinary but may lead to further action if attendance does not improve.

6. Statutory Sick Pay (SSP)

6.1 SSP Entitlement

  • Employees are entitled to SSP from the 4th consecutive day of sickness absence
  • Current SSP rate: £116.75 per week (2025 rate, subject to annual government changes)
  • SSP is paid for up to 28 weeks of sickness
  • To qualify, you must earn above the Lower Earnings Limit and follow the notification procedures

6.2 Company Sick Pay

TallTree Technologies offers enhanced sick pay arrangements:

  • First 6 months of employment: SSP only
  • 6 months - 2 years' service: Up to 2 weeks full pay, then SSP
  • 2+ years' service: Up to 4 weeks full pay, then 4 weeks half pay, then SSP

Enhanced sick pay is discretionary and subject to following correct notification procedures and providing medical certificates as required.

7. Parental Leave Rights

7.1 Maternity Leave

  • Entitlement: Up to 52 weeks (26 weeks Ordinary Maternity Leave + 26 weeks Additional Maternity Leave)
  • Notification: Must notify employer by the 15th week before the expected week of childbirth
  • Statutory Maternity Pay (SMP): 90% of average earnings for 6 weeks, then £184.03 per week (2025 rate) or 90% of average earnings (whichever is lower) for 33 weeks
  • Enhanced provision: Employees with 12+ months' service receive 6 weeks full pay, 12 weeks half pay + SMP, then SMP only

7.2 Paternity Leave

  • Entitlement: 1 or 2 weeks' leave (taken together, not separately)
  • Must be taken: Within 56 days of the birth or adoption placement
  • Statutory Paternity Pay (SPP): £184.03 per week (2025 rate) or 90% of average earnings (whichever is lower)
  • Enhanced provision: 2 weeks full pay for employees with 6+ months' service

7.3 Shared Parental Leave (SPL)

  • Allows parents to share up to 50 weeks of leave and 37 weeks of pay
  • Available if eligible for maternity/paternity leave and meet the employment and earnings criteria
  • Requires 8 weeks' notice and completion of necessary forms
  • Contact HR for detailed guidance on eligibility and process

7.4 Adoption Leave

  • Same entitlements as maternity leave for the primary adopter
  • Secondary adopter entitled to paternity leave
  • Notification required with matching certificate from adoption agency

7.5 Parental Leave

  • Entitlement: 18 weeks unpaid leave per child (up to their 18th birthday)
  • Eligibility: After 1 year's service
  • Usage: Maximum 4 weeks per year per child
  • Notice: 21 days before intended start date

7.6 Time Off for Dependants

  • Reasonable unpaid time off to deal with emergencies involving dependants
  • Examples: unexpected childcare breakdown, dealing with an incident at school, arranging longer-term care
  • Notify your manager as soon as possible
  • This is for emergencies, not planned situations

8. Compassionate Leave

8.1 Bereavement Leave

Employees are entitled to paid compassionate leave in the event of bereavement:

  • Immediate family (spouse, partner, child, parent, sibling): Up to 5 days paid leave
  • Other close family members: Up to 3 days paid leave
  • Additional leave: May be granted as unpaid leave or annual leave by agreement

8.2 Other Compassionate Circumstances

  • Paid leave may be granted for other serious family emergencies or circumstances
  • Each situation will be considered on its merits
  • Contact your line manager or HR as soon as possible to discuss your needs

8.3 Parental Bereavement Leave

  • Parents who lose a child under 18 or suffer stillbirth from 24 weeks of pregnancy are entitled to 2 weeks' paid parental bereavement leave
  • Can be taken as 1 block of 2 weeks or 2 separate weeks
  • Must be taken within 56 weeks of the death

9. Unpaid Leave

9.1 Requesting Unpaid Leave

  • Employees may request unpaid leave where annual leave entitlement is exhausted or for extended personal reasons
  • Requests should be made in writing to your line manager with as much notice as possible
  • Approval is at the company's discretion based on business needs
  • Extended unpaid leave (more than 1 week) requires HR approval

9.2 Career Breaks

  • Employees with 2+ years' service may request a career break of up to 6 months
  • Must apply at least 3 months in advance with business case
  • Subject to senior management approval
  • No guarantee of return to the same role, but reasonable efforts will be made
  • Terms will be set out in a written agreement

10. Public Holidays

10.1 UK Bank Holidays

The following 8 UK bank holidays are included in the 28-day annual leave entitlement:

  • New Year's Day
  • Good Friday
  • Easter Monday
  • Early May Bank Holiday
  • Spring Bank Holiday
  • Summer Bank Holiday
  • Christmas Day
  • Boxing Day

10.2 Working on Bank Holidays

  • The company is generally closed on bank holidays
  • If required to work on a bank holiday (exceptional circumstances), a day in lieu will be provided
  • This must be agreed in advance with your line manager

10.3 Bank Holiday Substitution

  • Employees may request to work on a bank holiday and take a different day off
  • Subject to business needs and manager approval
  • Must be agreed at least 2 weeks in advance

11. Other Types of Leave

11.1 Jury Service

  • Employees must notify their manager immediately upon receiving a jury summons
  • Time off will be granted as required by law (unpaid)
  • Employees can claim loss of earnings allowance from the court
  • Employees should return to work when not required at court

11.2 Medical Appointments

  • Routine appointments should be arranged outside working hours where possible
  • Where this is not possible, reasonable time off may be granted
  • Advance notice and appointment confirmation may be required
  • Antenatal appointments are protected by law (paid time off)

11.3 Study Leave

  • Time off for work-related study may be granted where it benefits the business
  • Must be agreed in advance with your line manager
  • Details will be set out in a learning agreement

11.4 Volunteer Days

  • TallTree Technologies encourages community engagement
  • Employees may request up to 2 days paid leave per year for volunteering activities
  • Subject to manager approval and business needs

12. Long Service

Employees who reach significant service milestones may be entitled to additional benefits:

  • 5 years' service: 1 additional day of annual leave (29 days total)
  • 10 years' service: 2 additional days (30 days total)

13. Leaving the Company

13.1 Accrued Leave

  • Employees who leave must take all accrued annual leave during their notice period where possible
  • Any unused leave will be paid in lieu in final salary
  • If more leave has been taken than accrued, the excess will be deducted from final pay

13.2 Notice Period Leave

  • Taking annual leave during notice period is subject to manager approval
  • The company may require employees to work their full notice period
  • Handover and transition activities may be prioritized

14. Records and Monitoring

  • All leave must be recorded in the company's leave management system
  • Employees are responsible for ensuring their leave records are accurate
  • Leave balances can be viewed at any time through the system
  • HR maintains central records of all absence for reporting and compliance purposes

15. Abuse of Leave Policies

  • False claims regarding sickness or other leave may result in disciplinary action
  • Taking unauthorized absence is a disciplinary matter
  • Patterns of absence or suspicious circumstances may be investigated
  • The company may require additional medical evidence where there are concerns

16. Policy Review

This policy will be reviewed annually and may be updated to reflect changes in legislation or best practices. Employees will be notified of any significant changes.

17. Questions and Support

If you have any questions about leave entitlements or procedures, please contact:

  • Your line manager for day-to-day leave requests and questions
  • HR department at hr@talltree.tech for policy questions, family leave, or complex situations
