Home Working Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2025

Policy Statement: TallTree Technologies operates as a fully remote company. This policy sets out the framework for home working arrangements, ensuring that all employees can work effectively, safely, and securely from their home locations.

1. Scope and Eligibility

This policy applies to all employees of TallTree Technologies. As a fully remote company, all roles are designed to be performed from home or another suitable remote location.

1.1 Eligibility

  • All permanent and contract employees are eligible for home working
  • New employees may begin working remotely from their first day, subject to successful completion of onboarding procedures
  • Employees must have the right to work in the UK
  • Employees must have a suitable home workspace that meets the requirements outlined in this policy

2. Workspace Requirements

Employees are responsible for maintaining a suitable workspace that enables them to perform their duties effectively and safely.

2.1 Minimum Requirements

  • A dedicated, quiet workspace suitable for professional work and video calls
  • Adequate lighting, heating, and ventilation
  • Ergonomic seating and desk setup (see Health and Safety Policy for guidance)
  • Sufficient space for company-provided equipment
  • A safe electrical installation with adequate power outlets
  • Privacy for handling confidential information and client communications

2.2 DSE Assessment

All employees must complete a Display Screen Equipment (DSE) self-assessment within their first month of employment and annually thereafter. The company will provide guidance and support to ensure workstations meet health and safety standards.

3. Equipment Provision

TallTree Technologies provides the necessary equipment for employees to work effectively from home.

3.1 Standard Equipment Provided

  • Laptop or desktop computer appropriate to the role
  • Monitor(s) as required for the role
  • Keyboard and mouse
  • Headset with microphone for calls and meetings
  • Webcam (if not integrated into laptop)
  • Necessary software licenses and access credentials

3.2 Additional Equipment

Additional equipment such as ergonomic chairs, desk accessories, or specialist software may be provided subject to business need and manager approval. Employees requiring reasonable adjustments should refer to the Reasonable Adjustments Policy.

3.3 Equipment Care and Responsibility

  • All company equipment remains the property of TallTree Technologies
  • Employees must take reasonable care of all equipment provided
  • Equipment should be used primarily for work purposes
  • Any equipment faults or damage must be reported immediately to IT support
  • All equipment must be returned upon termination of employment
  • Employees must ensure equipment is kept secure and not accessible to unauthorized persons

4. Internet and Connectivity Requirements

4.1 Internet Connection

Employees must have access to a reliable internet connection with the following minimum specifications:

  • Download speed: minimum 10 Mbps (25 Mbps recommended)
  • Upload speed: minimum 2 Mbps (5 Mbps recommended)
  • Stable connection suitable for video conferencing
  • Backup connectivity option recommended (e.g., mobile hotspot)

4.2 Broadband Allowance

TallTree Technologies provides a monthly home office allowance to contribute towards internet costs. Details are provided in employment contracts or through the HR department.

4.3 Connectivity Issues

Employees experiencing persistent connectivity issues should:

  • Notify their line manager immediately
  • Use alternative connectivity where possible (mobile hotspot)
  • Work with IT support to resolve issues
  • Consider temporary alternative working arrangements if the issue persists

5. Working Hours and Availability

5.1 Core Working Hours

Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM UK time, Monday to Friday, unless otherwise specified in the employment contract. Employees are expected to be available and responsive during these hours.

5.2 Flexibility

  • Reasonable flexibility is permitted with line manager agreement
  • Employees must ensure adequate coverage for their responsibilities
  • Core hours (10:00 AM - 4:00 PM) should generally be worked by all team members to ensure collaboration
  • Any alternative working patterns must be agreed in advance

5.3 Time Recording

Employees must accurately record their working hours in accordance with Working Time Regulations and company procedures.

5.4 Breaks

Employees are entitled to and encouraged to take regular breaks, including:

  • A minimum 30-minute unpaid lunch break
  • Regular short breaks away from the screen (minimum 5-10 minutes every hour recommended)
  • Breaks as required under the Working Time Regulations

6. Communication Expectations

Effective communication is essential for a successful remote working environment.

6.1 Communication Channels

  • Slack: Primary communication tool for instant messaging, team collaboration, and informal communications. Employees should be available on Slack during working hours.
  • Email: Used for formal communications, external correspondence, and matters requiring documentation. Emails should be responded to within one business day.
  • Video Calls: Regular team meetings, one-to-ones, and client calls. Cameras should be on where possible to facilitate engagement.
  • Phone: For urgent matters or where voice communication is more appropriate.

6.2 Response Time Expectations

  • Slack messages: within 2 hours during working hours
  • Emails: within 1 business day
  • Urgent matters: immediate response or escalation
  • Set status indicators (available, in a meeting, away) appropriately

6.3 Video Call Etiquette

  • Ensure camera is on where possible (exceptions for technical issues or personal circumstances)
  • Use appropriate professional background or virtual background
  • Mute when not speaking in large meetings
  • Dress professionally for external client calls
  • Test audio and video before important calls
  • Be punctual for scheduled meetings

6.4 Availability Status

Employees should maintain accurate availability status on communication platforms and calendars, indicating when they are:

  • Available for work
  • In a meeting
  • Away from desk (lunch, break)
  • On leave or unavailable

7. Data Security and GDPR Compliance

7.1 Information Security

All employees must comply with the company's information security policies and procedures:

  • Use strong, unique passwords for all systems and enable multi-factor authentication where available
  • Never share login credentials with others
  • Lock screens when away from the workspace
  • Use company-approved VPN when accessing company systems
  • Ensure WiFi networks are secured with strong passwords
  • Keep all software and systems up to date with security patches
  • Report any security incidents or suspected breaches immediately

7.2 Data Protection and GDPR

  • Handle all personal and confidential data in accordance with GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018
  • Store confidential documents securely and dispose of them appropriately (shredding)
  • Do not work on confidential matters in public spaces or where screens may be visible to others
  • Use encrypted communication channels for sensitive information
  • Do not store company data on personal devices unless explicitly authorized
  • Ensure household members and visitors cannot access confidential information

7.3 Physical Security

  • Keep all company equipment secure and out of sight when not in use
  • Ensure doors and windows are secured when working with sensitive information
  • Do not leave equipment in vehicles or unsecured locations
  • Report lost or stolen equipment immediately

8. Health and Safety Responsibilities

8.1 Employer Responsibilities

TallTree Technologies will:

  • Provide guidance on setting up a safe home workspace
  • Conduct DSE assessments and provide necessary adjustments
  • Provide appropriate equipment to support safe working
  • Maintain employer's liability insurance covering home working
  • Support employees' wellbeing through policies and resources

8.2 Employee Responsibilities

Employees must:

  • Complete DSE self-assessments and implement recommendations
  • Maintain a safe working environment
  • Report any health and safety concerns to their line manager
  • Take regular breaks and maintain good ergonomic practices
  • Report accidents or incidents in accordance with company procedures
  • Inform their home insurer that they are working from home

For detailed guidance, please refer to the Health and Safety Policy for Remote Workers.

9. Right to Work from Abroad

9.1 Short-Term Working Abroad

Employees may request to work temporarily from abroad subject to the following conditions:

  • Maximum period: 30 days in any 12-month period without specific approval
  • Written approval from line manager required in advance
  • Must be in a location with suitable internet connectivity
  • Working hours must align with UK business hours or be agreed in advance
  • Employee remains subject to UK employment law and tax
  • Must comply with all company policies including data security

9.2 Extended Working Abroad

Requests to work abroad for longer than 30 days require:

  • Written application to HR and line manager at least 8 weeks in advance
  • Assessment of tax, employment law, and data protection implications
  • Approval from senior management
  • Formal agreement documenting terms and conditions

9.3 Restrictions

  • The company reserves the right to refuse requests where there are business, legal, or practical concerns
  • Employees are responsible for ensuring they have the right to work in the proposed location
  • Working from high-risk countries or regions with data protection concerns may not be permitted

10. Expenses and Allowances

10.1 Home Office Allowance

TallTree Technologies provides a monthly home office allowance to contribute towards:

  • Internet and broadband costs
  • Heating and electricity
  • General home office expenses

Details of allowance amounts are provided in employment contracts.

10.2 Tax Relief

Employees may be eligible to claim tax relief for working from home costs. Employees should consult HMRC guidance or their tax advisor for information on eligibility and claiming procedures.

10.3 Expense Claims

Additional reasonable expenses incurred for business purposes may be claimed in accordance with the company's expense policy. This may include:

  • Additional equipment not provided by the company (subject to prior approval)
  • Specialist items required for reasonable adjustments
  • Business travel and accommodation
  • Professional subscriptions relevant to the role

11. Insurance Considerations

11.1 Employer's Liability Insurance

TallTree Technologies maintains employer's liability insurance covering employees working from home in the UK.

11.2 Employee Responsibilities

Employees must:

  • Notify their home contents and buildings insurer that they are working from home
  • Ensure adequate insurance coverage for company equipment in their home
  • Check whether their home insurance covers business equipment (the company's insurance is primary, but employee insurance may provide additional protection)
  • Notify the company if their insurer imposes any restrictions on home working

11.3 Damage or Loss

  • Employees should report any damage to or loss of company equipment immediately
  • The company's insurance will generally cover accidental damage to company equipment
  • Employees may be liable for damage resulting from negligence or misuse

12. Performance and Management

12.1 Performance Expectations

Home working does not change the performance standards expected of employees. All employees are expected to:

  • Deliver work to the same standard as if working in an office
  • Meet agreed deadlines and objectives
  • Participate fully in team activities and meetings
  • Maintain professional conduct and communication

12.2 Regular Check-ins

Line managers will conduct regular one-to-one meetings with remote employees to:

  • Discuss workload and priorities
  • Address any challenges or concerns
  • Provide feedback and support
  • Discuss wellbeing and work-life balance

13. Wellbeing and Work-Life Balance

TallTree Technologies is committed to supporting employee wellbeing and maintaining healthy work-life boundaries.

13.1 Right to Disconnect

  • Employees are not expected to respond to communications outside working hours
  • Scheduled emails outside working hours should use delayed send features
  • Employees should set boundaries between work and personal time

13.2 Mental Health Support

  • Employees are encouraged to take regular breaks and maintain social connections
  • Support resources are available through HR
  • Employees experiencing difficulties should speak with their line manager or HR

14. Review and Changes to Working Arrangements

This policy will be reviewed annually and may be updated to reflect changes in legislation, best practices, or business needs.

The company reserves the right to:

  • Require employees to work from a company office or other location where business needs dictate (with reasonable notice)
  • Withdraw or modify home working arrangements if they are not working effectively
  • Request visits to home workspaces for health and safety or equipment purposes (with reasonable notice and consent)

15. Questions and Support

If you have any questions about this policy or need support with your home working arrangements, please contact:

  • Your line manager for day-to-day working arrangements and equipment needs
  • HR department at hr@talltree.tech for policy questions and employment matters
  • IT support for technical issues and equipment problems
