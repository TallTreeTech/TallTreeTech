5. Response Times and Priorities

5.1 Incident Priority Definitions

Priority Definition Examples
Critical (P1) Complete service outage or critical functionality unavailable affecting all users or critical business operations Production system down, data breach, complete loss of service, security incident
High (P2) Major functionality degraded or unavailable affecting multiple users or important business functions Significant performance degradation, major feature not working, partial service disruption
Medium (P3) Minor functionality issue or inconvenience affecting limited users with workaround available Non-critical bug, minor performance issue, cosmetic problems with workaround
Low (P4) Minor issue with minimal impact, information request, or enhancement suggestion Feature requests, documentation updates, general questions, minor cosmetic issues

5.2 Response Time Commitments

Priority Initial Response Regular Updates Resolution Target
Critical (P1) 30 minutes Every 2 hours 4 hours (best effort)
High (P2) 2 hours Every 4 hours 8 business hours
Medium (P3) 8 business hours Daily 5 business days
Low (P4) 2 business days As needed 15 business days

Notes:

  • Initial Response: Acknowledgment of issue, assignment to engineer, and preliminary assessment

  • Business Hours: [9:00-18:00 GMT/BST, Monday-Friday, excluding UK public holidays]
  • After Hours: P1 and P2 incidents supported 24/7; P3 and P4 handled during business hours only

9. Remedies for SLA Breaches

9.1 Service Credits

If TallTree fails to meet the SLA commitments set forth in this agreement, Client may be eligible for service credits as follows:

Availability Credits:

Monthly Availability Service Credit
Below 99.9% but ≥99.5% 5% of monthly fees
Below 99.5% but ≥99.0% 10% of monthly fees
Below 99.0% but ≥98.0% 25% of monthly fees
Below 98.0% 50% of monthly fees

Response Time Credits:

Response Time Compliance Service Credit
Below 95% but ≥90% 5% of monthly fees
Below 90% but ≥85% 10% of monthly fees
Below 85% 15% of monthly fees

9.2 Service Credit Conditions

  • Service credits are calculated as a percentage of the monthly support fees for the affected month
  • Maximum combined service credits in any month: 50% of monthly fees
  • Service credits are the sole and exclusive remedy for SLA breaches
  • Credits must be requested in writing within 30 days of receiving monthly report
  • Credits issued as a discount on the next month's invoice, not cash refunds
  • No credits for downtime caused by Client actions, third-party failures, or force majeure

9.3 SLA Exclusions

SLA commitments do not apply during:

  • Scheduled maintenance windows with proper advance notice
  • Emergency maintenance to address critical security vulnerabilities
  • Client-caused issues (unauthorized changes, failure to follow procedures, etc.)
  • Third-party service provider outages beyond TallTree's control
  • Force majeure events
  • Network or infrastructure issues on Client's side
  • Beta features or services explicitly marked as "not covered by SLA"

9.4 Termination for Persistent SLA Failures

If TallTree fails to meet SLA commitments for three consecutive months:

  • Client may request an improvement plan within 10 business days
  • If SLA failures continue for two additional months after plan implementation, Client may terminate the agreement with 30 days' notice without penalty
