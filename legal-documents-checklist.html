Category 1: Client Agreements

1.1 Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) ✓ COMPLETED

HIGH PRIORITY

Purpose: Protects confidential information shared during prospect discussions and throughout client engagement.

When to use: First document sent to prospects when discussing potential projects. Remains in force until end of engagement.

File: mutual-nda.html

✓ Completed - UK-compliant mutual NDA with whistleblowing protections and 5-year term

1.2 Master Services Agreement (MSA) ✓ COMPLETED

HIGH PRIORITY

Purpose: Establishes overarching terms and conditions for ongoing client relationships.

When to use: For clients with multiple projects or ongoing relationships. Works in conjunction with SOWs.

Key clauses:

  • Service scope and limitations
  • Payment terms and invoicing
  • Mutual intellectual property ownership and licensing
  • Warranties and UCTA-compliant liability limitations
  • Governance and relationship management
  • 3-level escalation and dispute resolution
  • Anti-bribery and compliance provisions

✓ Completed - Comprehensive UK-compliant MSA with £100k general liability cap, £500k data/IP cap, £1M PI insurance

1.3 Statement of Work (SOW) Template ✓ COMPLETED

HIGH PRIORITY

Purpose: Defines specific project deliverables, timelines, and costs under an MSA.

When to use: For each individual project or engagement under an MSA.

Key sections:

  • Project scope, objectives, and deliverables
  • Timeline, milestones, and acceptance criteria
  • Fee breakdown and payment schedule
  • Client responsibilities and dependencies
  • Change control process
  • Project-specific terms (communication, working arrangements)

1.4 Retainer Agreement PENDING

HIGH PRIORITY

Purpose: Governs fractional and retainer-based consulting arrangements.

When to use: For clients engaging you on a fractional basis (e.g., fractional CTO/CISO) or monthly retainer.

Key clauses:

  • Monthly retainer fee and included hours
  • Scope of services and responsibilities
  • Overage rates for additional hours
  • Response time commitments
  • Renewal and termination terms
  • Conflict of interest provisions
Needed for: Fractional clients and retainer-based clients

1.5 Project Contract Agreement PENDING

HIGH PRIORITY

Purpose: Standalone agreement for one-off projects (alternative to MSA + SOW).

When to use: For single, discrete projects where an MSA is not necessary.

Key clauses:

  • Complete project scope
  • Fixed price or time & materials terms
  • Payment schedule (milestones or phases)
  • Deliverables and acceptance
  • IP ownership
  • Warranties and limitations
Needed for: One-off project clients without ongoing relationship

Category 2: Employment Documents

2.1 Employment Contract PENDING

HIGH PRIORITY

Purpose: Legal employment agreement for all employees (required by UK law).

When to use: Must be provided to all employees within 2 months of start date (Statement of Particulars).

Key clauses:

  • Job title and duties
  • Salary and benefits
  • Working hours and location
  • Holiday entitlement
  • Notice periods
  • Pension provisions
  • Probation period
  • Confidentiality obligations
Legally required for: All employees under UK employment law

2.2 Employee Confidentiality & IP Assignment Agreement PENDING

HIGH PRIORITY

Purpose: Protects company IP and ensures work created by employees belongs to the company.

When to use: Signed at start of employment, often incorporated into employment contract.

Key clauses:

  • Confidentiality obligations
  • IP assignment provisions
  • Post-employment restrictions
  • Return of company property
  • Non-solicitation (if applicable)
Needed for: All employees, especially those creating IP or accessing confidential information

2.3 Employee Handbook PENDING

MEDIUM PRIORITY

Purpose: Sets out company policies, procedures, and expectations.

When to use: Provided to all employees, updated regularly.

Key sections:

  • Code of conduct
  • Health and safety
  • Grievance and disciplinary procedures
  • Equal opportunities policy
  • Data protection policy
  • Social media policy
  • Remote working policy
  • Whistleblowing policy
Needed for: Creating consistent workplace culture and legal compliance

2.4 Employee Privacy Notice PENDING

HIGH PRIORITY

Purpose: GDPR-compliant notice explaining how employee data is processed.

When to use: Must be provided before or at the start of employment (GDPR requirement).

Key sections:

  • What data is collected
  • Legal basis for processing
  • How data is used
  • Data retention periods
  • Employee rights under GDPR
  • Data sharing and transfers
Legally required for: GDPR compliance for all employee data processing

2.5 Disciplinary & Grievance Procedures PENDING

MEDIUM PRIORITY

Purpose: Formal procedures for handling workplace issues and complaints.

When to use: When addressing performance issues or employee grievances.

Key elements:

  • Disciplinary process stages
  • Grievance raising procedure
  • Investigation protocols
  • Appeal process
  • ACAS code compliance
Needed for: Legal protection and fair treatment of employees

Category 3: Data Protection & Privacy

3.1 Privacy Policy ✓ COMPLETED

HIGH PRIORITY

Purpose: GDPR-compliant policy explaining how customer/visitor data is collected and used.

When to use: Published on website, referenced in client contracts.

File: privacy.html

✓ Completed - GDPR-compliant privacy policy for website and services

3.2 Data Processing Agreement (DPA) PENDING

HIGH PRIORITY

Purpose: GDPR-required agreement when processing personal data on behalf of clients.

When to use: When your consulting services involve processing client personal data (required by GDPR Article 28).

Key clauses:

  • Scope and purpose of processing
  • Data processor obligations
  • Sub-processor provisions
  • Security measures
  • Data breach notification
  • Data subject rights assistance
  • Data deletion/return
  • Audit rights
Legally required when: Acting as data processor for client personal data

3.3 Cookie Policy PENDING

MEDIUM PRIORITY

Purpose: Explains cookie usage on website (PECR/GDPR requirement).

When to use: Published on website if using cookies.

Key sections:

  • Types of cookies used
  • Purpose of each cookie
  • How to manage cookies
  • Third-party cookies
  • Cookie consent mechanism
Needed if: Website uses cookies (analytics, functional, marketing)

3.4 Data Breach Response Plan PENDING

MEDIUM PRIORITY

Purpose: Internal procedure for responding to data breaches (GDPR requirement).

When to use: In the event of a personal data breach.

Key sections:

  • Breach identification and assessment
  • Containment procedures
  • ICO notification process (72-hour requirement)
  • Data subject notification
  • Documentation requirements
  • Post-breach review
Needed for: GDPR compliance and rapid breach response

3.5 Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) Template PENDING

LOW PRIORITY

Purpose: Template for assessing data protection risks in high-risk processing activities.

When to use: Before starting processing that's likely to result in high risk to individuals' rights.

Key sections:

  • Description of processing
  • Necessity and proportionality assessment
  • Risk identification
  • Mitigation measures
  • Consultation records
Needed for: High-risk data processing activities (security, IoT implementations)

Category 4: Intellectual Property

4.1 Client IP Assignment Agreement PENDING

HIGH PRIORITY

Purpose: Clarifies IP ownership for work created during consulting engagements.

When to use: Typically incorporated into MSA or project contracts.

Key clauses:

  • Custom work IP assignment to client
  • Pre-existing IP retention
  • License grants for tools/frameworks
  • Third-party IP handling
  • Moral rights waiver
Needed for: Clarity on who owns deliverables (especially software, designs, documentation)

4.2 Software License Agreement PENDING

MEDIUM PRIORITY

Purpose: Licenses proprietary tools, frameworks, or software to clients.

When to use: When providing clients with license to use your proprietary software or tools.

Key clauses:

  • License grant (scope, exclusivity, territory)
  • Usage restrictions
  • License fees
  • Maintenance and support terms
  • Warranties and disclaimers
  • Termination rights
Needed if: You have proprietary tools/platforms you license to clients

4.3 Open Source Compliance Policy PENDING

LOW PRIORITY

Purpose: Internal policy for using open source software in client projects.

When to use: Guides developers on acceptable open source usage.

Key sections:

  • Approved open source licenses
  • Prohibited licenses (e.g., GPL in commercial projects)
  • License compatibility checks
  • Attribution requirements
  • Client disclosure obligations
Needed for: Managing open source risk in software development projects

Category 6: Contractor & Subcontractor Agreements

6.1 Contractor Agreement (B2B) PENDING

HIGH PRIORITY

Purpose: Engages independent contractors/freelancers (business-to-business).

When to use: When hiring contractors to augment your team.

Key clauses:

  • Services to be provided
  • Fees and payment terms
  • IR35 status determination
  • Contractor warranties (right to work, insurance)
  • IP assignment to your company
  • Confidentiality obligations
  • Termination rights
Needed if: Using freelancers or contractors for client projects

6.2 Subcontractor Agreement PENDING

MEDIUM PRIORITY

Purpose: Engages subcontractors to fulfill client obligations.

When to use: When subcontracting part of client work to third parties.

Key clauses:

  • Scope aligned with client contract
  • Flow-down of client obligations
  • Confidentiality (protecting client information)
  • IP assignment chain
  • Quality standards
  • Indemnification
  • Client approval requirements
Needed if: Subcontracting work to deliver on client contracts

6.3 IR35 Status Determination Statement PENDING

HIGH PRIORITY

Purpose: Documents IR35 assessment for contractors (UK tax compliance).

When to use: Before engaging contractors, as required by UK tax law for medium/large businesses.

Key sections:

  • Status determination (inside or outside IR35)
  • Reasoning and factors considered
  • CEST tool results (if used)
  • Right to challenge process
  • Tax responsibility allocation
Legally required for: IR35 compliance when engaging contractors (if medium/large company)

Category 7: Business Operations

7.1 Non-Compete Agreement PENDING

LOW PRIORITY

Purpose: Restricts employees/contractors from competing after engagement ends.

When to use: For senior employees or contractors with access to sensitive information.

Key clauses:

  • Geographic scope
  • Time limitation (typically 3-12 months)
  • Restricted activities
  • Consideration (must be reasonable)
  • Severability

⚠️ Warning: Non-competes are difficult to enforce in UK and must be very reasonable.

Use with caution: Only for senior roles where truly necessary and reasonable

7.2 Settlement Agreement Template PENDING

LOW PRIORITY

Purpose: Legally binding agreement to settle employment disputes.

When to use: When terminating employment or settling disputes with employees.

Key clauses:

  • Settlement payment details
  • Waiver of claims
  • Confidentiality provisions
  • Reference agreement
  • Independent legal advice requirement
  • Tax treatment of payments
Needed for: Managed exits and dispute resolution with employees

7.3 Complaints Handling Procedure PENDING

MEDIUM PRIORITY

Purpose: Formal process for handling client complaints.

When to use: When clients raise service issues or complaints.

Key sections:

  • How to submit complaints
  • Acknowledgment timeframes
  • Investigation process
  • Resolution and escalation
  • Record keeping
  • Learning and improvement
Needed for: Professional complaint management and client satisfaction

Next Steps

Recommended Actions:

  1. Review Completed Documents: Review the mutual NDA, MSA, and SOW template to ensure they meet your needs
  2. Core Framework Complete: You now have the essential framework (NDA + MSA + SOW) for client engagements. Consider these next priorities:
  3. Engage Legal Counsel: Have a UK solicitor review all documents, especially:
    • Client contracts (high financial risk)
    • Employment documents (high legal compliance risk)
    • Data processing agreements (GDPR compliance)
  4. Customize for Your Business: Tailor each document to your specific services and business model
  5. Implement Document Management: Set up a system for version control and tracking signed agreements
  6. Train Your Team: Ensure employees understand key policies and procedures
  7. Regular Review: Review and update all documents annually or when laws change
