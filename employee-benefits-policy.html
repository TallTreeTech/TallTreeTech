Employee Benefits Policy

Software Systems Limited trading as TallTree Technologies

Last updated: November 14, 2025

Our Commitment: The Company values our employees and offers a comprehensive benefits package designed to support wellbeing, professional development, and work-life balance.

1. Overview

This policy outlines the benefits available to employees of the Company. Benefits are designed to:

  • Support employee health and wellbeing
  • Provide financial security and peace of mind
  • Enable professional growth and development
  • Promote work-life balance and flexibility
  • Recognize and reward contributions

2. Statutory Benefits

The Company complies with all UK statutory employment benefits:

2.1 Workplace Pension

Auto-enrolment Pension Scheme

  • Scheme provider: [Pension provider name]
  • Eligibility: Automatic enrolment for eligible employees (aged 22 to State Pension age, earning over £10,000 per year)
  • Employee contribution: Minimum 5% of qualifying earnings
  • Employer contribution: Minimum 3% of qualifying earnings (total 8%)
  • Enhanced employer contribution: The Company may contribute above the minimum for longer-serving employees or senior positions
  • Opt-out rights: Employees may opt out but will be re-enrolled every three years as required by law

Pension advice: The Company recommends seeking independent financial advice before making pension decisions.

2.2 Statutory Sick Pay (SSP)

  • Rate: £116.75 per week (2025/26 rate, updated annually)
  • Eligibility: After 4 consecutive days of illness (including non-working days)
  • Duration: Up to 28 weeks
  • Requirements: Self-certification for up to 7 days; medical certificate (fit note) thereafter

Note: See section 3.1 for enhanced Company Sick Pay

2.3 Statutory Maternity Leave and Pay

  • Maternity leave: Up to 52 weeks (26 weeks Ordinary Maternity Leave + 26 weeks Additional Maternity Leave)
  • Statutory Maternity Pay (SMP):
    • First 6 weeks: 90% of average weekly earnings
    • Remaining 33 weeks: £184.03 per week or 90% of earnings (whichever is lower)
  • Eligibility: 26 weeks' continuous employment by end of 15th week before due date

Note: See section 3.2 for enhanced Maternity Pay

2.4 Statutory Paternity Leave and Pay

  • Paternity leave: 1 or 2 weeks (taken consecutively)
  • Statutory Paternity Pay (SPP): £184.03 per week or 90% of earnings (whichever is lower)
  • When taken: Within 56 days of birth or adoption
  • Eligibility: 26 weeks' continuous employment by end of 15th week before due date

Note: See section 3.3 for enhanced Paternity Pay

2.5 Shared Parental Leave and Pay

  • Leave: Up to 50 weeks shared between parents (taken in blocks)
  • Pay: Up to 37 weeks at £184.03 per week or 90% of earnings (whichever is lower)
  • Eligibility: Both parents must meet qualifying criteria
  • Notice requirements: 8 weeks' notice required for each period of leave

2.6 Adoption Leave and Pay

  • Leave: Up to 52 weeks
  • Pay: Same structure as Statutory Maternity Pay
  • Eligibility: 26 weeks' continuous employment by matching date

2.7 Parental Leave

  • Entitlement: 18 weeks unpaid leave per child (up to child's 18th birthday)
  • Usage: Maximum 4 weeks per year per child
  • Eligibility: 1 year's continuous employment

2.8 Time Off for Dependants

  • Entitlement: Reasonable unpaid time off to deal with emergencies involving dependants
  • Examples: Child's illness, breakdown of care arrangements, death of dependant

2.9 Annual Leave

  • Statutory minimum: 5.6 weeks (28 days for full-time employees, including bank holidays)
  • Company allowance: See section 3.4

3. Enhanced Company Benefits

The Company offers enhanced benefits above statutory minimums:

3.1 Enhanced Sick Pay

Company Sick Pay Scheme

Length of Service Full Pay Half Pay SSP Only
Less than 1 year 1 week 1 week Remainder up to 28 weeks
1-3 years 4 weeks 4 weeks Remainder up to 28 weeks
3-5 years 8 weeks 8 weeks Remainder up to 28 weeks

Notes:

  • Enhanced sick pay is in addition to SSP
  • Medical certificates required after 7 consecutive days
  • Absence managed in accordance with Absence Management Policy
  • Discretion may be exercised in exceptional circumstances

3.2 Enhanced Maternity Pay

Enhanced Maternity Benefits

  • Eligibility: 12 months' continuous employment by due date
  • Enhanced pay:
    • Weeks 1-6: 90% of average weekly earnings (statutory)
    • Weeks 7-18: Full pay (Company enhancement)
    • Weeks 19-39: £184.03 per week or 90% of earnings (whichever is lower)
    • Weeks 40-52: Unpaid
  • Return to work: Expected to return for minimum 6 months or repay enhanced element
  • Keeping in Touch (KIT) days: Up to 10 paid days during maternity leave

3.3 Enhanced Paternity Leave

Enhanced Paternity Benefits

  • Eligibility: 6 months' continuous employment
  • Enhanced leave: 4 weeks (instead of statutory 2 weeks)
  • Enhanced pay: Full pay for all 4 weeks
  • Timing: Must be taken within 56 days of birth/adoption
  • Can be taken: As single block or two blocks of 2 weeks

3.4 Enhanced Annual Leave

Company Annual Leave Entitlement

  • Base entitlement: 25 days per year (full-time equivalent) PLUS 8 bank holidays
  • Total: 33 days per year (5.6 weeks above statutory minimum)
  • Service enhancement:
    • After 5 years' service: +2 days (27 days + bank holidays)
    • After 10 years' service: +3 days (28 days + bank holidays)
  • Pro-rata: Part-time employees receive pro-rata entitlement
  • Carry-over: Up to 5 days may be carried to next year (with approval)
  • Purchase scheme: Option to purchase up to 5 additional days (unpaid)

3.5 Birthday Leave

  • Entitlement: 1 additional day off on or around your birthday
  • Eligibility: All employees after probation period
  • Not deducted: Does not count against annual leave allowance

3.6 Study Leave

  • Entitlement: Up to 5 days per year for study or professional exams
  • Conditions: Study must be relevant to role or career development
  • Approval required: Line manager and HR approval
  • Exam leave: Additional time for professional certification exams

4. Remote Work Equipment Allowance

4.1 Home Office Setup

The Company provides equipment and allowances to support effective remote working:

Company-Provided Equipment

  • Laptop/desktop computer (suitable for role)
  • Monitor(s) - up to 2 external monitors
  • Keyboard and mouse
  • Headset/microphone for meetings
  • Webcam (if not built-in)
  • Docking station/USB hub (if needed)

Ergonomic Equipment

  • Office chair - budget up to £300
  • Desk - budget up to £250 (or standing desk up to £400)
  • Laptop stand
  • Ergonomic accessories (wrist rests, footrests, etc.) - up to £50

One-Time Home Office Grant

  • Amount: £500 on commencement
  • Purpose: Additional equipment, lighting, storage, etc.
  • Receipts required: For reimbursement

4.2 Ongoing Home Office Allowance

  • Monthly allowance: £40 per month (tax-free under HMRC guidance)
  • Purpose: Contribution to heating, electricity, internet
  • Paid: Automatically with salary
  • Eligibility: Employees working from home 3+ days per week

4.3 Broadband Support

  • Reimbursement: Up to £50/month for business-grade broadband
  • Requirements: Minimum 50 Mbps download, reliable connection
  • Backup: 4G/5G mobile hotspot provided for critical roles

4.4 Equipment Maintenance and Replacement

  • IT support provided for all company equipment
  • Replacement/upgrades as needed for role effectiveness
  • Standard laptop refresh cycle: 3 years
  • Monitor refresh cycle: 5 years

5. Professional Development Budget

5.1 Annual Learning Budget

Each employee has an annual professional development budget:

  • Standard allocation: £2,000 per year
  • Senior roles: Up to £3,500 per year
  • Use-it-or-lose-it: Does not roll over to next year

5.2 Eligible Expenses

The budget can be used for:

  • Professional certifications and exams (AWS, Azure, Kubernetes, etc.)
  • Online courses and training platforms (Pluralsight, Udemy, Coursera, etc.)
  • Conference attendance and tickets
  • Professional association memberships
  • Books and learning materials
  • Workshops and bootcamps
  • Coaching or mentoring programs

5.3 Approval Process

  1. Discuss proposed training with line manager
  2. Ensure relevance to role or career development
  3. Submit request via HR system
  4. Receive approval before booking
  5. Retain receipts for reimbursement

5.4 Conference Attendance

  • Conferences: Encouraged for professional development and networking
  • Budget includes: Registration, travel, accommodation (within policy)
  • Time: Conference time counts as working time, not annual leave
  • Knowledge sharing: Share learnings with team on return

5.5 Professional Membership Fees

The Company pays for relevant professional memberships:

  • BCS (British Computer Society)
  • IET (Institution of Engineering and Technology)
  • ISACA
  • ISC² (for security professionals)
  • Other relevant professional bodies (with approval)

6. Flexible Working Arrangements

6.1 Right to Request Flexible Working

All employees have the statutory right to request flexible working from day one of employment.

6.2 Types of Flexible Working

Remote-First Culture

  • Default: Work from anywhere in the UK
  • Office space: Available in London (Bell Yard) for collaboration/meetings
  • Flexibility: Choose your work location daily

Flexible Hours

  • Core hours: 10:00-16:00 (when team should be generally available)
  • Flexible start/finish: Start between 7:00-10:00, finish between 16:00-19:00
  • Compressed hours: E.g., full-time hours over 4 days

Part-Time Working

  • Various part-time patterns available
  • Pro-rata salary and benefits
  • Minimum 0.6 FTE (3 days/week) for most roles

Job Shares

  • Two people sharing one full-time role
  • Subject to finding suitable partner and business needs

Term-Time Working

  • Unpaid leave during school holidays
  • Salary spread across 12 months or paid during term time only

6.3 Requesting Flexible Working

  1. Submit written request to line manager and HR
  2. Include proposed pattern and start date
  3. Discuss at meeting within 28 days
  4. Decision within 2 months
  5. Right to appeal if refused

See the Company's Flexible Working Policy for full details
