Diversity, Equity and Inclusion Policy

Software Systems Limited trading as TallTree Technologies

Last updated: November 14, 2025

Our Commitment: The Company is committed to creating and maintaining a diverse, equitable, and inclusive workplace where all employees, contractors, clients, and stakeholders are valued, respected, and empowered to contribute their best work.

1. Policy Statement

The Company believes that diversity strengthens our organisation and enriches our work environment. We are committed to:

  • Promoting equality of opportunity in all aspects of employment
  • Creating an inclusive culture where differences are valued
  • Eliminating unlawful discrimination, harassment, and victimisation
  • Ensuring fair treatment regardless of protected characteristics
  • Advancing equality and fostering good relations between people
  • Making reasonable adjustments to remove barriers

2. Legal Framework

This policy ensures compliance with UK equality legislation, including:

  • Equality Act 2010 - Protection against discrimination
  • Human Rights Act 1998 - Fundamental rights and freedoms
  • Employment Rights Act 1996 - Employment protections
  • Public Sector Equality Duty - Where applicable to clients

3. Protected Characteristics

The Equality Act 2010 protects individuals from discrimination based on nine protected characteristics. The Company is committed to ensuring that no employee or applicant receives less favourable treatment on the basis of:

The Nine Protected Characteristics

1. Age

We do not discriminate based on age. We value the contributions of employees at all life and career stages, from early career professionals to experienced senior staff.

  • No upper or lower age limits in recruitment (except legal minimums)
  • Age-diverse teams and mentoring programs
  • Training and development opportunities for all ages
  • Flexible retirement options

2. Disability

We are committed to creating an accessible workplace for people with physical, mental, or sensory disabilities, including learning difficulties and long-term health conditions.

  • Reasonable adjustments in recruitment and employment
  • Accessible technology and remote working arrangements
  • Adjustments to working hours, duties, or equipment
  • Mental health support and understanding
  • Regular reviews of accessibility needs

3. Gender Reassignment

We support transgender and non-binary employees and applicants throughout any transition process or gender identity journey.

  • Respect for chosen names and pronouns
  • Privacy and confidentiality maintained
  • Support during transition periods (time off, phased return)
  • Appropriate facilities and documentation updates
  • Zero tolerance for transphobia

4. Marriage and Civil Partnership

We do not discriminate based on marital or civil partnership status.

  • Equal treatment regardless of relationship status
  • Equal benefits for married and civil partners
  • No assumptions about availability or commitment

5. Pregnancy and Maternity

We support employees through pregnancy, maternity, and return to work.

  • No discrimination during pregnancy or maternity leave
  • Enhanced maternity benefits (see Employee Benefits Policy)
  • Risk assessments and adjustments during pregnancy
  • Flexible return to work arrangements
  • Keeping in Touch (KIT) days available
  • Protection from detriment related to maternity

6. Race

We embrace racial and ethnic diversity including colour, nationality, ethnic or national origins.

  • Ethnically diverse recruitment and progression
  • Zero tolerance for racism or racial harassment
  • Cultural awareness and celebration of diversity
  • Monitoring of ethnicity pay gaps and representation
  • Support for international employees and visa sponsorship

7. Religion or Belief

We respect all religions and philosophical beliefs, as well as lack of religion or belief.

  • Flexible working for religious observance
  • Respect for dietary requirements and dress codes
  • Appropriate facilities where feasible (e.g., prayer space)
  • Holiday arrangements accommodating different faiths
  • Protection for atheists and those with philosophical beliefs

8. Sex (Gender)

We are committed to gender equality for men, women, and non-binary individuals.

  • Equal pay for equal work (see Pay Equity section)
  • Gender-balanced recruitment and promotion
  • Shared parental leave support
  • Challenging gender stereotypes and bias
  • Gender pay gap monitoring and action
  • Support for women in technology

9. Sexual Orientation

We support and celebrate LGBTQ+ employees and create an inclusive environment for all sexual orientations.

  • LGBTQ+ inclusive language and policies
  • Equal benefits for same-sex partners
  • Zero tolerance for homophobia, biphobia, or related discrimination
  • Support networks and allies
  • Recognition of Pride and LGBTQ+ History Month

4. Recruitment and Hiring Practices

4.1 Inclusive Recruitment

The Company ensures fair and inclusive recruitment processes:

  • Job descriptions: Free from discriminatory language, focus on essential requirements
  • Advertising: Diverse channels to reach broad talent pools
  • Application process: Accessible formats and reasonable adjustments available
  • Shortlisting: Based solely on merit against job criteria
  • Interviews: Structured questions, diverse interview panels where possible
  • Anonymous recruitment: Removing identifying information to reduce bias (where appropriate)

4.2 Positive Action

Where permitted by law, the Company may take positive action to:

  • Encourage applications from underrepresented groups
  • Provide targeted training or development opportunities
  • Use as a tie-breaker between equally qualified candidates
Note: Positive action is not the same as positive discrimination (quotas), which remains unlawful. All hiring decisions are based on merit.

4.3 Right to Work

The Company does not discriminate on grounds of nationality or immigration status, but does:

  • Conduct lawful right to work checks for all employees (see UK Sponsor License Policy)
  • Support visa sponsorship for eligible roles
  • Provide equal treatment for sponsored workers

5. Pay Equity and Compensation

5.1 Equal Pay

The Company is committed to equal pay for equal work:

  • Objective, gender-neutral job evaluation frameworks
  • Regular pay equity audits across protected characteristics
  • Transparent salary bands and progression criteria
  • Elimination of unexplained pay gaps

5.2 Pay Gap Monitoring

The Company monitors and reports on pay gaps:

  • Gender pay gap: Annual internal review (published if required by law)
  • Ethnicity pay gap: Voluntary monitoring and action plans
  • Disability pay gap: Monitoring and addressing barriers
  • Action plans: Addressing any identified disparities

5.3 Pay Decisions

All pay decisions are based on:

  • Job requirements, skills, and experience
  • Performance and contribution
  • Market rates and internal equity
  • Never on protected characteristics

6. Inclusive Workplace Culture

6.1 Creating Inclusion

The Company fosters inclusion through:

  • Leadership commitment: Visible commitment from senior management
  • Employee voice: Regular feedback mechanisms and employee forums
  • Inclusive language: Gender-neutral and respectful communication
  • Cultural awareness: Celebrating diversity through events and recognition
  • Flexible working: Accommodating different needs and circumstances
  • Remote-first culture: Ensuring equal participation for remote workers

6.2 Preventing Discrimination

The Company has zero tolerance for:

  • Direct discrimination: Less favourable treatment due to protected characteristic
  • Indirect discrimination: Policies or practices that disadvantage certain groups
  • Harassment: Unwanted conduct related to protected characteristics
  • Victimisation: Detriment for making or supporting a complaint
  • Microaggressions: Subtle but harmful comments or behaviours

6.3 Challenging Bias

All employees are expected to:

  • Recognise and challenge their own unconscious biases
  • Speak up against discriminatory behaviour
  • Support colleagues experiencing discrimination
  • Contribute to an inclusive culture

7. Reasonable Adjustments

7.1 Legal Duty

Under the Equality Act 2010, the Company has a legal duty to make reasonable adjustments for disabled employees and applicants.

7.2 Types of Adjustments

Reasonable adjustments may include:

  • Physical adjustments: Accessible office spaces, ergonomic equipment, assistive technology
  • Working arrangements: Flexible hours, remote working, adjusted duties, phased returns
  • Policies and procedures: Modified absence policies, alternative communication methods
  • Support: Job coaching, mentoring, additional training time

7.3 Assessment Process

  1. Employee discloses disability or adjustment need
  2. Confidential discussion with line manager and HR
  3. Assessment of individual needs (occupational health input if needed)
  4. Identification of reasonable adjustments
  5. Implementation and review of adjustments
  6. Regular reviews and updates as needs change
Note: See also the Company's Reasonable Adjustments Policy for detailed procedures.

8. Training and Development

8.1 Equality and Diversity Training

All employees receive training on:

  • Induction: DE&I policy, protected characteristics, expected behaviours
  • Annual refresher: Updates, case studies, unconscious bias
  • Manager training: Inclusive leadership, reasonable adjustments, handling complaints
  • Specialist training: Mental health awareness, neurodiversity, cultural competence

8.2 Career Development

The Company ensures equal access to development opportunities:

  • Fair allocation of training, projects, and stretch assignments
  • Mentoring and sponsorship programs
  • Transparent promotion criteria and processes
  • Support for underrepresented groups in leadership
  • Professional development budgets for all employees

9. Monitoring and Reporting

9.1 Workforce Data

The Company collects and monitors diversity data (with consent) on:

  • Recruitment and hiring (applications, shortlisting, offers)
  • Workforce composition by protected characteristics
  • Promotion and progression rates
  • Pay gaps and compensation equity
  • Training and development participation
  • Retention and turnover rates
  • Disciplinary and grievance cases

9.2 Data Protection

  • All diversity data collected and processed lawfully under UK GDPR
  • Voluntary disclosure - employees not required to provide diversity information
  • Data stored securely and confidentially
  • Used only for equality monitoring and policy effectiveness
  • Reported in aggregate form only to protect individual privacy

9.3 Reporting and Action

The Company:

  • Reviews diversity metrics quarterly
  • Publishes annual diversity report (internal)
  • Sets measurable diversity objectives
  • Takes action to address underrepresentation or disparities
  • Reports to senior management and board

10. Complaints and Grievances

10.1 Raising Concerns

Employees who experience or witness discrimination, harassment, or inequality should:

Step 1: Informal Resolution (where appropriate)
  • Speak directly to the individual if comfortable doing so
  • Explain the impact of their behaviour
  • Request that the behaviour stops
Step 2: Speak to Your Manager
  • Raise concerns with your line manager
  • Manager will take appropriate action and may involve HR
Step 3: Formal Grievance
  • Submit written grievance to HR: hr@talltree.tech
  • Formal investigation will be conducted
  • See the Company's Grievance Procedure for full process
Step 4: External Reporting
  • ACAS (Advisory, Conciliation and Arbitration Service): 0300 123 1100
  • Equality and Human Rights Commission (EHRC): www.equalityhumanrights.com
  • Employment Tribunal (usually within 3 months of incident)

10.2 Investigation Process

All complaints are taken seriously and investigated promptly:

  • Confidential investigation by trained investigator
  • Both parties given opportunity to present their case
  • Witnesses interviewed as appropriate
  • Findings and recommendations documented
  • Appropriate action taken if discrimination found
  • Support provided to complainant throughout process

10.3 Protection from Victimisation

The Company prohibits victimisation of anyone who:

  • Makes a complaint of discrimination or harassment
  • Supports a colleague's complaint
  • Provides evidence in an investigation
  • Raises equality concerns
Protection: Victimisation is itself unlawful discrimination and will be treated as a serious disciplinary matter.

10.4 Disciplinary Action

Discrimination, harassment, or victimisation may result in:

  • Formal warning
  • Final written warning
  • Dismissal (including for first offence if gross misconduct)
  • Report to police (for criminal offences)

11. Roles and Responsibilities

11.1 Senior Management

  • Demonstrate visible commitment to DE&I
  • Set diversity objectives and allocate resources
  • Monitor progress and hold leaders accountable
  • Ensure policy is embedded in business strategy

11.2 Line Managers

  • Model inclusive behaviours
  • Ensure team compliance with this policy
  • Address discrimination or bias promptly
  • Support reasonable adjustments
  • Ensure fair access to opportunities
  • Create psychologically safe team environment

11.3 All Employees

  • Treat colleagues, clients, and partners with respect
  • Comply with this policy and related procedures
  • Challenge discrimination and bias
  • Participate in DE&I training
  • Report concerns or incidents
  • Support inclusive culture

11.4 HR Team

  • Implement and maintain DE&I policies and procedures
  • Provide guidance and support to employees and managers
  • Monitor diversity data and metrics
  • Conduct training and awareness programs
  • Investigate complaints and grievances
  • Report on progress to senior management

12. Policy Review

This policy is reviewed:

  • Annually: Scheduled comprehensive review
  • Following incidents: After any discrimination findings
  • Legislative changes: When equality law is updated
  • Best practice: To reflect evolving DE&I standards
