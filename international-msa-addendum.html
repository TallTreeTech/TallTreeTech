2.3 Foreign Exchange and Currency Conversion

Where services are quoted in GBP but payment is made in a different currency:

  • Exchange Rate: The exchange rate shall be the Bloomberg mid-market rate (or if unavailable, the daily European Central Bank reference rate) as of 12:00 noon London time on the invoice date;
  • Currency Risk: The Client bears all currency exchange risk. If payment is delayed beyond the payment terms and currency fluctuation exceeds 3%, TallTree reserves the right to re-invoice at the current exchange rate;
  • Conversion Costs: All bank charges, wire transfer fees, and foreign exchange conversion fees shall be borne by the Client such that TallTree receives the full invoiced amount;
  • Fixed Rate Option: Upon request and mutual agreement, the Parties may agree to fix exchange rates for the duration of a Statement of Work, subject to a currency risk adjustment fee.

2.4 International Payment Methods

The Client may make payment by the following methods:

  • SWIFT Wire Transfer: To TallTree's UK bank account (details provided on invoice). Client bears all intermediary and beneficiary bank charges;
  • SEPA Transfer: For EUR payments from EU clients (no intermediary fees within SEPA zone);
  • International Payment Platforms: Wise, PayPal, or similar (subject to 2-3% processing fee for amounts over equivalent of £1,000);
  • Credit/Debit Card: For amounts under equivalent of £5,000 (subject to 2.5% processing fee).

2.5 VAT and International Taxation

UK VAT Treatment:

  • UK Clients: UK VAT at 20% applies to all services;
  • EU Business Clients: Reverse charge mechanism applies under Article 44 of the EU VAT Directive (as applicable to UK-EU transactions). Client must provide valid VAT registration number. No UK VAT charged; client self-accounts for VAT in their jurisdiction;
  • Non-EU International Clients: Services are treated as exports. No UK VAT charged (outside the scope of UK VAT);
  • EU Consumer Clients: UK VAT may apply depending on nature of services and Brexit transition provisions.

Withholding Tax:

  • If the Client's jurisdiction requires withholding tax on payments for services, the Client shall:
    • Gross up the payment so that TallTree receives the full invoiced amount;
    • Provide TallTree with official tax receipts/certificates evidencing the withholding;
    • Cooperate with TallTree to claim relief under applicable tax treaties;
  • TallTree may provide tax residency certificates or other documentation to support reduced withholding rates under double taxation treaties.

Sales Tax / GST (Non-UK):

  • US Clients: Client is responsible for reporting and remitting any applicable state sales tax or use tax. TallTree does not collect US sales tax;
  • Australian Clients: If TallTree is required to register for Australian GST, GST will be added at 10%. Otherwise, client may be required to account for GST under reverse charge;
  • Other Jurisdictions: Client is responsible for local tax compliance unless otherwise agreed in writing.

4. Export Control Compliance

4.1 Export Control Laws

Each Party shall comply with all applicable export control and trade sanctions laws and regulations, including but not limited to:

  • UK Export Control Act 2002 and Export Control Order 2008;
  • EU Dual-Use Regulation (EU) 2021/821;
  • US Export Administration Regulations (EAR) and International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR), to the extent applicable;
  • UN, UK, EU, and US sanctions programs.

4.2 Restricted Parties and Jurisdictions

Each Party represents and warrants that it is not:

  • Listed on any government restricted parties list (including OFAC SDN List, UK Consolidated List, EU Sanctions List);
  • Located in, organized under the laws of, or owned/controlled by persons in, a comprehensively sanctioned jurisdiction;
  • Engaged in activities prohibited under applicable export control or sanctions laws.

4.3 Technology Transfers

TallTree shall:

  • Classify any deliverables containing controlled technology under applicable export control regimes;
  • Obtain necessary export licenses if required before transferring controlled technology;
  • Notify the Client if any deliverables are subject to export control restrictions;
  • Include appropriate end-use and re-export restrictions in deliverables documentation.

The Client shall:

  • Not re-export or transfer any controlled technology without appropriate authorizations;
  • Ensure end-users comply with applicable export control requirements;
  • Provide end-use statements or other documentation if requested by TallTree for export license applications.

5. Regional Variations

5.1 European Union Specific Provisions

For clients located in EU member states, the following additional provisions apply:

  • Data Protection: Full compliance with EU GDPR (Regulation 2016/679) and execution of Standard Contractual Clauses for UK-EU data transfers (if required post-Brexit adequacy arrangements);
  • Consumer Protection: For business-to-consumer services, compliance with EU Consumer Rights Directive and Distance Selling Regulations;
  • Late Payment: Late payment interest in accordance with Directive 2011/7/EU on combating late payment in commercial transactions (8% above ECB reference rate);
  • VAT: Reverse charge mechanism for B2B services under Article 44 of EU VAT Directive;
  • Digital Services: Compliance with EU Digital Services Act and e-Commerce Directive as applicable;
  • Liability: Acknowledgment that EU consumer protection laws may override certain limitation of liability provisions for consumer contracts.

5.2 United States Specific Provisions

For clients located in the United States, the following additional provisions apply:

  • Governing Law: If US law is selected under Clause 1.1, the specific state law shall be designated (commonly Delaware or New York for commercial contracts);
  • Payment Terms: Standard Net 30 payment terms in accordance with US commercial practice;
  • Currency: Invoicing in USD is standard for US clients;
  • Sales Tax: TallTree does not collect US state sales tax; Client is responsible for reporting use tax as applicable;
  • Export Controls: Compliance with US EAR and ITAR to the extent TallTree's services or deliverables are subject to US jurisdiction;
  • Limitation of Liability: Certain limitations may be subject to state law variations; consequential damages exclusions generally enforceable;
  • Electronic Signatures: Acknowledgment that electronic signatures are valid under the US Electronic Signatures in Global and National Commerce Act (E-SIGN Act, 15 U.S.C. § 7001);
  • Arbitration: If AAA arbitration is selected, conducted under AAA Commercial Arbitration Rules;
  • Discovery: If US litigation applies, parties acknowledge broader discovery obligations under US Federal Rules of Civil Procedure.

For further US-specific variations, refer to the separate US MSA Variant document.

5.3 Australia Specific Provisions

For clients located in Australia, the following additional provisions apply:

  • Australian Consumer Law: Nothing in this Agreement excludes, restricts, or modifies any consumer guarantee, right, or remedy conferred by the Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth) or the Australian Securities and Investments Commission Act 2001 (Cth) that cannot be lawfully excluded;
  • Limitation of Liability: To the extent permitted by law, TallTree's liability for breach of non-excludable consumer guarantees is limited to, at TallTree's option:
    • Re-supply of the services; or
    • Payment of the cost of having the services supplied again;
  • GST: Fees are exclusive of Australian GST. If GST applies, it will be added to invoices at the prevailing rate (currently 10%);
  • Privacy: Compliance with Australian Privacy Act 1988 and Privacy Principles for handling of personal information;
  • Payment Terms: Typically 14-30 days net, in AUD or USD as agreed;
  • Unfair Contract Terms: Both parties acknowledge the unfair contract terms provisions in Australian Consumer Law and agree that this Agreement has been negotiated in good faith.

6. Time Zones and Service Delivery

6.1 Time Zone References

Unless otherwise specified in a Statement of Work:

  • Primary Time Zone: All times referenced in this Agreement and Statements of Work are in British Summer Time (BST) or Greenwich Mean Time (GMT), as applicable (collectively "UK Time");
  • Business Hours: TallTree's standard business hours are 09:00-17:30 UK Time, Monday-Friday, excluding UK public holidays;
  • Deadlines: All deadlines, due dates, and time-sensitive obligations are calculated in UK Time unless expressly stated otherwise.

6.2 International Time Zone Support

For international clients, the Parties may agree in a Statement of Work to:

  • Extended support hours to accommodate the Client's time zone;
  • Scheduled meetings at mutually convenient times across time zones;
  • Response time commitments adjusted for time zone differences;
  • Use of the Client's local time zone for project milestones and deliverables;
  • Any additional fees for services provided outside TallTree's standard UK business hours.

The Parties agree to:

  • Conduct meetings via video conference to accommodate international locations;
