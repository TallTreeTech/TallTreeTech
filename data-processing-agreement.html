Background and Scope

A. Relationship to Master Agreement

This DPA is entered into in connection with and forms part of the Master Services Agreement dated [Date] between the Parties (the "MSA"). This DPA supplements the MSA and governs the processing of personal data by TallTree on behalf of the Client.

B. Legal Basis

This DPA satisfies the requirement under Article 28(3) GDPR and UK GDPR for a written contract between controller and processor. In the event of any conflict between this DPA and the MSA, this DPA shall prevail with respect to data processing matters.

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 GDPR Definitions

In this DPA, the following terms have the meanings given to them in the GDPR and UK GDPR:

  • "Controller" means the natural or legal person which determines the purposes and means of the processing of personal data;
  • "Data Subject" means an identified or identifiable natural person;
  • "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person;
  • "Personal Data Breach" means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, personal data;
  • "Processing" means any operation performed on personal data, whether or not by automated means;
  • "Processor" means a natural or legal person which processes personal data on behalf of the controller;
  • "Sub-processor" means any processor engaged by the Processor to process personal data on behalf of the Controller;
  • "Supervisory Authority" means the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) in the UK, or the relevant data protection authority in an EU member state.

1.2 Applicable Law

  • "UK GDPR" means the UK General Data Protection Regulation as transposed into UK law under the Data Protection Act 2018 and the European Union (Withdrawal) Act 2018;
  • "EU GDPR" means Regulation (EU) 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council;
  • "Data Protection Laws" means the UK GDPR, EU GDPR (as applicable), Data Protection Act 2018, Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations, and all other applicable data protection and privacy laws.

2. Processing of Personal Data

2.1 Roles and Scope

The Parties acknowledge and agree that:

  • The Client is the Controller of the Personal Data;
  • TallTree is the Processor of the Personal Data;
  • TallTree shall process Personal Data only on behalf of and in accordance with the documented instructions of the Controller;
  • The scope, nature, and purpose of processing are described in Schedule 1 (Details of Processing);
  • The duration of processing is for the term of the MSA and applicable Statements of Work, plus any retention period required by law or agreed by the Parties.

2.2 Controller Instructions

Article 28(3)(a) GDPR: The processor shall process personal data only on documented instructions from the controller.

TallTree shall:

  • Process Personal Data only in accordance with the Client's documented written instructions, unless required to do so by applicable law;
  • Immediately inform the Client if, in TallTree's opinion, an instruction infringes the Data Protection Laws;
  • Not process Personal Data for TallTree's own purposes or on behalf of any third party without the Client's prior written consent.

The Client's initial instructions are set out in this DPA and the MSA. Further instructions may be provided by the Client in writing from time to time.

2.3 Compliance with Data Protection Laws

Each Party shall comply with its respective obligations under the Data Protection Laws. TallTree shall assist the Client in ensuring compliance with the Client's obligations under the Data Protection Laws, taking into account the nature of processing and the information available to TallTree.

4. Processor Obligations

4.1 Confidentiality

Article 28(3)(b) GDPR: The processor shall ensure that persons authorized to process personal data are subject to confidentiality obligations.

TallTree shall:

  • Ensure that all personnel authorized to process Personal Data are subject to a duty of confidentiality (whether by contract or statutory obligation);
  • Provide appropriate training to personnel on data protection requirements;
  • Ensure that access to Personal Data is limited to personnel who need access to perform the Services.

4.2 Security of Processing

Article 32 GDPR: The processor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk.

TallTree shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including as appropriate:

  • Pseudonymization and encryption of personal data;
  • Ongoing confidentiality, integrity, availability, and resilience of processing systems and services;
  • Ability to restore availability and access to personal data in a timely manner in the event of a physical or technical incident;
  • Regular testing, assessment, and evaluation of the effectiveness of technical and organizational measures;
  • Measures to identify vulnerabilities with regard to processing personal data in systems used to provide the Services.

The security measures implemented by TallTree are detailed in Schedule 2 (Technical and Organizational Measures).

4.3 Sub-processing

Article 28(3)(d) GDPR: The processor shall not engage another processor without prior authorization from the controller.

4.3.1 General Authorization

The Client provides general written authorization for TallTree to engage Sub-processors, subject to the conditions set out in this Clause 4.3.

4.3.2 List of Sub-processors

TallTree's current list of Sub-processors is set out in Schedule 3 (Sub-processors). This list includes:

  • The Sub-processor's name and location;
  • A description of the processing activities performed;
  • The jurisdiction in which the Sub-processor is established.

4.3.3 Notification and Objection

TallTree shall:

  • Notify the Client (by email to the contact specified in this DPA) of any intended changes concerning the addition or replacement of Sub-processors;
  • Provide such notification at least thirty (30) days before authorizing the new Sub-processor to process Personal Data;
  • Provide the Client with sufficient information to enable the Client to exercise its right to object.

The Client may object to the appointment of a new Sub-processor on reasonable grounds relating to data protection. If the Client objects, the Parties shall work together in good faith to find a commercially reasonable solution. If no such solution can be found, either Party may terminate the affected Statement of Work without penalty.

4.3.4 Sub-processor Requirements

TallTree shall:

  • Impose on each Sub-processor data protection obligations equivalent to those in this DPA, by way of a written contract;
  • Remain fully liable to the Client for the performance of any Sub-processor's obligations;
  • Ensure Sub-processors comply with the obligations in this DPA.

4.4 International Data Transfers

Chapter V GDPR: Transfers of personal data to third countries must be subject to appropriate safeguards.

4.4.1 Restricted Transfers

TallTree shall not transfer Personal Data outside the United Kingdom or European Economic Area (EEA) without ensuring that appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with Chapter V of the GDPR and UK GDPR.

4.4.2 Transfer Mechanisms

Where Personal Data is transferred to a third country, TallTree shall ensure one of the following safeguards applies:

  • The recipient country is subject to an adequacy decision by the UK Government or European Commission;
  • The transfer is governed by Standard Contractual Clauses approved under Article 46(2)(c) GDPR:
    • EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Commission Decision 2021/914); or
    • UK International Data Transfer Addendum (IDTA) to the EU SCCs; or
    • UK International Data Transfer Agreement (as applicable);
  • The recipient has Binding Corporate Rules approved by a competent supervisory authority;
  • The transfer is covered by an approved certification mechanism;
  • A derogation under Article 49 GDPR applies (to be used only in exceptional circumstances).

4.4.3 Incorporation of Standard Contractual Clauses

Where applicable, the Parties agree to execute and be bound by:

  • The EU Standard Contractual Clauses (2021/914) for controller-to-processor transfers (Module Two); and/or
  • The UK International Data Transfer Addendum (IDTA) to the EU SCCs, version B1.0;

Such Standard Contractual Clauses or IDTA are hereby incorporated by reference and shall be deemed executed by both Parties. The details required under the SCCs/IDTA are set out in Schedule 4 (Transfer Impact Assessment and SCCs Details).

4.4.4 Transfer Impact Assessment

TallTree has conducted a Transfer Impact Assessment for transfers to third countries and has implemented appropriate supplementary measures where necessary. A summary of the assessment is provided in Schedule 4.

5. Data Subject Rights

5.1 Assistance with Data Subject Requests

Article 28(3)(e) GDPR: The processor shall assist the controller in responding to data subject rights requests.

TallTree shall, taking into account the nature of the processing, provide reasonable assistance to the Client to enable the Client to respond to requests from Data Subjects exercising their rights under the Data Protection Laws, including:

  • Right of access (Article 15);
  • Right to rectification (Article 16);
  • Right to erasure / "right to be forgotten" (Article 17);
  • Right to restriction of processing (Article 18);
  • Right to data portability (Article 20);
  • Right to object (Article 21);
  • Rights related to automated decision-making and profiling (Article 22).

5.2 Procedure for Data Subject Requests

If TallTree receives a Data Subject request directly:

  • TallTree shall promptly (and in any event within two (2) Working Days) notify the Client of the request;
  • TallTree shall not respond to the Data Subject directly without the Client's prior written authorization;
  • TallTree shall provide reasonable cooperation and assistance to the Client in responding to the request;
  • If the Client instructs TallTree to take action in response to a Data Subject request, TallTree shall do so in accordance with the Client's written instructions within a reasonable timeframe.

5.3 Fees for Assistance

TallTree's reasonable assistance under this Clause 5 is included in the Fees under the MSA. If a Data Subject request requires extensive or complex work by TallTree beyond normal cooperation, TallTree may charge additional fees on a time-and-materials basis at TallTree's prevailing rates, subject to prior agreement with the Client.

6. Personal Data Breaches

6.1 Notification to Controller

Article 33 GDPR: A controller must notify a personal data breach to the supervisory authority without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours.

TallTree shall notify the Client without undue delay, and in any event within 24 hours, upon becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting the Client's Personal Data.

The notification shall include, to the extent known:

  • A description of the nature of the Personal Data Breach, including where possible the categories and approximate number of Data Subjects and personal data records concerned;
  • The name and contact details of TallTree's data protection officer or other contact point;
  • A description of the likely consequences of the Personal Data Breach;
  • A description of the measures taken or proposed to be taken to address the breach, including measures to mitigate its possible adverse effects;
  • Where applicable, a timeline of the breach and discovery.

6.2 Cooperation and Remediation

TallTree shall:

  • Cooperate with the Client and provide reasonable assistance in the Client's handling of the Personal Data Breach;
  • Take reasonable steps to mitigate the effects of the breach and prevent further breaches;
  • Provide ongoing updates to the Client as further information about the breach becomes available;
  • Preserve evidence relating to the breach for investigation and regulatory purposes;
  • Not notify any Data Subjects, regulators, or third parties of the breach without the Client's prior written consent, unless required by law.

6.3 Controller's Notification Obligations

The Parties acknowledge that the Client, as Controller, is responsible for determining whether notification to the Supervisory Authority or Data Subjects is required under Articles 33 and 34 GDPR. TallTree's notification to the Client under this Clause 6 does not relieve the Client of its own notification obligations.

7. Data Protection Impact Assessment and Prior Consultation

7.1 Assistance with DPIA

Article 28(3)(f) GDPR: The processor shall assist the controller in ensuring compliance with obligations regarding data protection impact assessments and prior consultation.

Where a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA) is required under Article 35 GDPR, TallTree shall provide reasonable assistance and cooperation to the Client, including:

  • Providing information about the nature, scope, context, and purposes of processing;
  • Describing the technical and organizational security measures implemented;
  • Assisting in assessing risks to data subjects;
  • Providing information about Sub-processors and international transfers.

7.2 Prior Consultation

If the Client is required to consult with a Supervisory Authority under Article 36 GDPR (prior consultation), TallTree shall provide reasonable assistance and information to facilitate such consultation.

8. Deletion or Return of Personal Data

8.1 End of Processing

Article 28(3)(g) GDPR: At the end of the provision of services, the processor shall delete or return all personal data to the controller.

Upon termination or expiration of the MSA, or upon the Client's written request, TallTree shall, at the Client's election:

  • Return: Return all Personal Data to the Client in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format; or
  • Delete: Securely delete or destroy all Personal Data in TallTree's possession or control, including all copies and backups.

8.2 Exceptions

TallTree may retain Personal Data to the extent required by applicable law (e.g., tax, employment, or other legal obligations). Any Personal Data retained shall remain subject to the confidentiality and security obligations in this DPA and shall be deleted as soon as the legal retention period expires.

8.3 Certification

Upon the Client's written request, TallTree shall provide written certification that it has complied with the deletion or return obligations under this Clause 8.

9. Audits and Inspections

9.1 Right to Audit

Article 28(3)(h) GDPR: The processor shall make available to the controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance and allow for audits.

TallTree shall:

  • Make available to the Client all information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA and the Data Protection Laws;
  • Allow for and contribute to audits and inspections conducted by the Client or an independent auditor mandated by the Client.

9.2 Audit Procedure

The Client may conduct audits subject to the following conditions:

  • Notice: The Client shall provide TallTree with at least thirty (30) days' prior written notice of any proposed audit;
  • Frequency: Audits shall not occur more than once per calendar year, except:
    • Following a Personal Data Breach;
    • At the direction of a Supervisory Authority; or
    • Where the Client has reasonable grounds to believe TallTree is not complying with this DPA;
  • Scope: Audits shall be limited to TallTree's processing of the Client's Personal Data and relevant security and data protection controls;
  • Timing: Audits shall be conducted during TallTree's normal business hours and in a manner that does not unreasonably interfere with TallTree's business operations;
  • Confidentiality: The Client and any auditors shall execute appropriate confidentiality agreements;
  • Costs: The Client shall bear the costs of audits, including reasonable reimbursement to TallTree for time and resources expended (unless the audit reveals material non-compliance by TallTree, in which case TallTree shall bear the costs).

9.3 Alternative Compliance Evidence

In lieu of an on-site audit, the Client may accept alternative evidence of compliance, including:

  • TallTree's ISO 27001 certification or other relevant security certifications;
  • Third-party audit reports (SOC 2, Cyber Essentials Plus, or equivalent);
  • Completed information security questionnaires;
  • Written attestations from TallTree's management or data protection officer.

10. Records of Processing Activities

10.1 Processor's Records

Article 30(2) GDPR: Each processor shall maintain a record of all categories of processing activities carried out on behalf of a controller.

TallTree shall maintain written records of all categories of processing activities carried out on behalf of the Client, containing:

  • The categories of processing carried out on behalf of each Controller;
  • Where applicable, transfers of personal data to a third country, including identification of the country and documentation of safeguards;
  • A general description of the technical and organizational security measures.

10.2 Availability to Supervisory Authority

TallTree shall make its records of processing activities available to the Supervisory Authority upon request.

12. Liability and Indemnity

12.1 Processor Liability

Article 82 GDPR: Any person who has suffered material or non-material damage as a result of an infringement of GDPR shall have the right to receive compensation.

The Parties acknowledge that:

  • Under Article 82 GDPR, Data Subjects may bring claims for compensation against Controllers or Processors;
  • TallTree may be held liable for damage caused by processing only where it has not complied with obligations specifically directed to processors under GDPR, or where it has acted outside or contrary to lawful instructions of the Controller;
  • The liability provisions in the MSA (Clause 8) apply to this DPA, subject to the requirements of Data Protection Laws.

12.2 GDPR Fines and Penalties

To the extent that either Party is subject to fines, penalties, or sanctions imposed by a Supervisory Authority as a result of the other Party's breach of this DPA or Data Protection Laws:

  • The breaching Party shall indemnify the non-breaching Party for such fines, penalties, or sanctions;
  • Subject to the liability caps in the MSA (as permitted by law);
  • Provided that the non-breaching Party cooperates with the breaching Party in defending against or mitigating such fines or penalties.

13. Term and Termination

13.1 Term

This DPA shall commence on the date of signature and shall remain in effect for the duration of the MSA and any Statements of Work that involve processing of Personal Data, plus any retention period required by law or this DPA.

13.2 Effect of Termination

Upon termination of this DPA, TallTree shall comply with the deletion or return obligations in Clause 8.

13.3 Survival

The following provisions shall survive termination: Clauses 6 (Personal Data Breaches), 8 (Deletion or Return), 9 (Audits), 10 (Records), and 12 (Liability).

14. General Provisions

14.1 Governing Law

This DPA shall be governed by the same law as the MSA. For the avoidance of doubt, this DPA does not change the Parties' obligations under Data Protection Laws, which apply regardless of the governing law of the contract.

14.2 Amendments

The Parties may amend this DPA by mutual written agreement to address changes in Data Protection Laws or regulatory guidance. TallTree may update the Schedules (Sub-processor list, security measures) in accordance with the procedures set out in this DPA.

14.3 Precedence

In the event of conflict between this DPA and the MSA regarding data protection matters, this DPA shall prevail. In the event of conflict between this DPA and mandatory requirements of Data Protection Laws, the Data Protection Laws shall prevail.

14.4 Severability

If any provision of this DPA is held invalid or unenforceable, that provision shall be construed in a manner to make it enforceable to the maximum extent permitted by law, and the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.
