Data Protection Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2025

This policy sets out how Software Systems Limited, trading as TallTree Technologies, complies with data protection legislation and manages personal data.

1. Legal Framework

This policy ensures compliance with:

  • UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)
  • Data Protection Act 2018 (DPA 2018)
  • Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR)
  • Relevant sector-specific regulations

We are committed to protecting the rights and privacy of individuals in accordance with these laws.

2. Scope and Application

Who This Policy Applies To

  • All employees, contractors, and consultants
  • Directors and officers
  • Third-party service providers with access to personal data
  • Any person processing data on our behalf

What Data This Covers

This policy applies to all personal data processed by the organisation, including:

  • Client and customer information
  • Employee and contractor data
  • Supplier and partner information
  • Website visitor data
  • Marketing and communication data
  • Technical and security logs

3. Data Protection Principles

We process all personal data in accordance with the following principles. Personal data must be:

  1. Lawfulness, fairness and transparency: Processed lawfully, fairly, and in a transparent manner
  2. Purpose limitation: Collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes
  3. Data minimisation: Adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary
  4. Accuracy: Accurate and kept up to date
  5. Storage limitation: Kept only as long as necessary
  6. Integrity and confidentiality: Processed securely with appropriate protection
  7. Accountability: We are responsible for and can demonstrate compliance

4. Lawful Basis for Processing

We only process personal data where we have a lawful basis. Our lawful bases include:

Contract

Processing is necessary for the performance of a contract with the data subject or to take steps at their request before entering into a contract.

  • Delivering consulting services
  • Managing client projects
  • Processing payments

Legal Obligation

Processing is necessary to comply with legal obligations.

  • Tax and accounting requirements
  • Employment law compliance
  • Regulatory reporting

Legitimate Interests

Processing is necessary for legitimate interests pursued by us or a third party (unless overridden by the rights of the data subject).

  • Business operations and administration
  • Fraud prevention and security
  • Direct marketing to business contacts

Consent

The data subject has given clear, informed consent for specific purposes.

  • Marketing communications (where required)
  • Special category data (where applicable)
  • Cookies and tracking technologies

5. Data Subject Rights

We respect and facilitate the following data subject rights:

Right to be Informed

  • Clear privacy notices explaining our data processing
  • Information provided at point of collection

Right of Access

  • Individuals can request copies of their personal data
  • We respond within 1 month (can be extended by 2 months for complex requests)
  • No fee charged unless requests are manifestly unfounded or excessive

Right to Rectification

  • Individuals can request correction of inaccurate data
  • We respond within 1 month

Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten)

  • Individuals can request deletion in certain circumstances
  • Subject to legal retention requirements

Right to Restrict Processing

  • Individuals can request limitation of processing in certain situations
  • Data can be stored but not further processed

Right to Data Portability

  • Individuals can request their data in machine-readable format
  • Applies to data provided under consent or contract

Right to Object

  • To processing based on legitimate interests
  • To direct marketing (absolute right)
  • To automated decision-making and profiling

How to Exercise Rights

Requests should be sent to: privacy@talltree.tech

6. Data Security Measures

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data:

Technical Measures

  • Encryption: Data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Access controls: Role-based access with principle of least privilege
  • Authentication: Multi-factor authentication for all systems
  • Network security: Firewalls, intrusion detection, VPNs
  • Endpoint protection: Antivirus, anti-malware, device encryption
  • Backups: Regular encrypted backups with tested restoration procedures
  • Monitoring: Security logging and monitoring of all systems

Organisational Measures

  • Policies and procedures: Documented data protection processes
  • Training: Regular data protection training for all staff
  • Clean desk policy: No personal data left unsecured
  • Secure disposal: Secure deletion and physical destruction procedures
  • Vendor management: Due diligence on all data processors
  • Incident response: Documented breach response procedures

7. Data Breach Procedures

In the event of a personal data breach, we follow these procedures:

Detection and Reporting

  • All staff must report suspected breaches immediately to: security@talltree.tech
  • Maximum 24 hours for internal reporting

Assessment (Within 24 hours)

  • Determine scope and severity of breach
  • Identify affected data subjects
  • Assess risk to individuals' rights and freedoms
  • Contain the breach and prevent further loss

Notification to ICO

  • Notify ICO within 72 hours if breach poses risk to individuals
  • Include description of breach, likely consequences, and measures taken

Notification to Data Subjects

  • Notify affected individuals without undue delay if high risk to their rights
  • Provide clear information about the breach and recommended actions

Documentation and Review

  • Document all breaches in breach register
  • Conduct post-incident review
  • Implement corrective measures

8. Privacy by Design and Default

We embed data protection into all our processes:

Privacy by Design

  • Data protection considered at the start of all projects
  • Privacy Impact Assessments (PIAs) for high-risk processing
  • Minimise data collection and retention
  • Pseudonymisation and anonymisation where possible

Privacy by Default

  • Default settings provide highest privacy protection
  • Only necessary data processed by default
  • Data not made publicly accessible without explicit action

9. Data Retention Schedules

We only retain personal data as long as necessary for the purposes collected:

Data Type Retention Period Legal Basis
Client contracts and project files 6 years after completion Limitation Act 1980
Accounting records 6 years after tax year end Tax and Companies Act requirements
Employee records 6 years after employment ends Employment law requirements
Marketing consent Until withdrawn or 3 years inactive Best practice
Website analytics 26 months ICO guidance
Security logs 12 months Security requirements
Complaint records 6 years after closure Limitation Act 1980

After retention periods expire, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

10. Third-Party Processors

When we engage third parties to process personal data on our behalf:

Due Diligence

  • Assess processor's security measures and compliance
  • Review data protection policies and certifications
  • Check financial stability and reputation

Contractual Requirements

  • Written Data Processing Agreement (DPA) required
  • Must include UK GDPR Article 28 requirements
  • Specify purpose, duration, and nature of processing
  • Define processor obligations and our rights
  • Address sub-processing, security, breach notification

Ongoing Oversight

  • Regular review of processor compliance
  • Audit rights and inspections where appropriate
  • Monitor for security incidents

11. International Transfers

When transferring personal data outside the UK:

Adequacy Decisions

  • Transfers to countries with UK adequacy decisions are permitted
  • Currently includes EU/EEA states and specified countries

Transfer Mechanisms (No Adequacy)

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs): UK International Data Transfer Agreement (IDTA)
  • Binding Corporate Rules (BCRs): For intra-group transfers
  • Certifications: Approved certification mechanisms

Transfer Impact Assessment

  • Assess laws and practices in destination country
  • Evaluate whether SCCs can be effective
  • Implement supplementary measures if needed
  • Document the assessment

12. Training Requirements

All personnel receive appropriate data protection training:

Induction Training

Annual Refresher Training

Role-Specific Training

Training Records

  • Maintain records of all training completed
  • Track compliance with training requirements
  • Identify training needs and gaps
