11. Dispute Resolution and Escalation

11.1 Escalation Process

In the event of any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement or any Statement of Work, or the breach, termination, or validity thereof ("Dispute"), the Parties agree to follow this escalation procedure before initiating formal dispute resolution:

Level 1 - Operational Discussion (Days 1-7):

  • The points of contact designated in Clause 10.1 shall meet (in person or virtually) within five (5) Working Days of either Party notifying the other of a Dispute;
  • The points of contact shall attempt to resolve the Dispute through good faith negotiation.

Level 2 - Management Escalation (Days 8-21):

  • If the Dispute is not resolved at Level 1 within seven (7) Working Days, either Party may escalate the matter to senior management;
  • Senior representatives of each Party with authority to settle the Dispute shall meet within five (5) Working Days of escalation;
  • The senior representatives shall attempt to resolve the Dispute through good faith negotiation.

Level 3 - Executive Escalation (Days 22-35):

  • If the Dispute is not resolved at Level 2 within fourteen (14) Working Days of escalation to Level 2, either Party may escalate to executive level;
  • An executive officer of each Party shall meet within five (5) Working Days of escalation;
  • The executives shall attempt to resolve the Dispute through good faith negotiation.

11.2 Mediation

If the Dispute is not resolved through the escalation process within thirty-five (35) Working Days of the initial notification of the Dispute, either Party may refer the matter to mediation by giving written notice to the other Party.

The Parties shall:

  • Attempt to agree on a mediator within ten (10) Working Days;
  • If unable to agree, request appointment of a mediator by the Centre for Effective Dispute Resolution (CEDR);
  • Participate in good faith in the mediation;
  • Share the costs of mediation equally.

11.3 Litigation

If the Dispute is not resolved through mediation within sixty (60) days of the commencement of mediation, either Party may commence litigation proceedings in accordance with Clause 20.2.

11.4 Continuing Obligations

Except where a Party has exercised rights of suspension or termination in accordance with this Agreement, both Parties shall continue to perform their respective obligations under this Agreement and any active Statements of Work during the pendency of any Dispute.

11.5 Urgent Relief
