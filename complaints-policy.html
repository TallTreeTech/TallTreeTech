Complaints Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2025

This policy sets out how Software Systems Limited, trading as TallTree Technologies, handles complaints from clients and stakeholders.

1. Purpose and Scope

We are committed to providing high-quality professional services. When things go wrong, we want to know about it so we can:

  • Put things right for you
  • Learn from the experience
  • Improve our services

This policy applies to all complaints regarding our consulting services, deliverables, conduct, or business practices.

2. How to Make a Complaint

You can submit a complaint through any of the following channels:

  • Email: complaints@talltree.tech
  • Post: Complaints Department, Software Systems Limited, 7 Bell Yard, London, England, WC2A 2JR
  • In writing: To your assigned project manager or consultant

Information to Include

To help us address your complaint effectively, please provide:

  • Your name and contact details
  • Your company or organisation name
  • Details of the service or project concerned
  • A clear description of your complaint
  • What happened and when
  • How you would like us to resolve the matter
  • Any supporting documentation

3. Complaint Receipt and Acknowledgment

We will acknowledge receipt of your complaint within 48 hours (2 business days).

Our acknowledgment will include:

  • A unique complaint reference number
  • The name of the person handling your complaint
  • Expected timeframes for our response
  • Information about our complaints process

4. Investigation Process

Once we receive your complaint, we will:

Initial Assessment (Days 1-3)

  • Assign the complaint to an appropriate senior staff member
  • Review all relevant documentation and correspondence
  • Identify any immediate actions needed
  • Determine the complexity and severity of the complaint

Investigation (Days 4-10)

  • Interview relevant staff members
  • Review project records, communications, and deliverables
  • Consult with technical experts if required
  • Identify root causes and contributing factors
  • Contact you if we need additional information

Resolution Development (Days 11-14)

  • Determine appropriate remedies or corrective actions
  • Prepare our formal response
  • Identify lessons learned and process improvements

5. Response Timeframes

Standard Response: We will provide a full written response within 14 calendar days of receiving your complaint.

Complex Complaints

If your complaint is particularly complex and requires more detailed investigation, we may need longer to investigate fully. In such cases:

  • We will contact you within the initial 14 days
  • We will explain why we need more time
  • We will provide a revised timeframe (typically not exceeding 28 days)
  • We will keep you updated on progress

6. Escalation to Senior Management

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can escalate your complaint to senior management.

How to Escalate

  • Email: senior.management@talltree.tech
  • Reference your original complaint number
  • Explain why you are dissatisfied with our response
  • State what outcome you are seeking

Senior Management Review

A senior director will:

  • Review your complaint and our previous response
  • Conduct an independent assessment
  • Respond within 14 calendar days
  • Provide a final decision on the matter

7. Resolution and Remedies

Depending on the nature and validity of your complaint, we may offer:

Service Remedies

  • Completion or correction of deliverables at no additional cost
  • Additional consulting hours to address deficiencies
  • Reassignment of personnel
  • Enhanced project oversight and quality assurance

Financial Remedies

  • Refund of fees for unsatisfactory services
  • Credit towards future services
  • Waiver of charges for remedial work
  • Compensation for direct costs incurred (where appropriate)

Process Remedies

  • Apology and acknowledgment of errors
  • Changes to our processes to prevent recurrence
  • Additional training for staff
  • Revised service delivery approach

8. Record Keeping

We maintain comprehensive records of all complaints, including:

  • Complaint details and supporting documentation
  • Investigation notes and findings
  • Communications with the complainant
  • Actions taken and outcomes
  • Timeframes and compliance with our policy

Complaint records are retained for 6 years in accordance with our record retention policy and applicable regulations.

9. Learning and Improvement

We treat complaints as opportunities for improvement. Our process includes:

Analysis

  • Quarterly review of all complaints
  • Identification of trends and patterns
  • Root cause analysis
  • Assessment of systemic issues

Action

  • Implementation of process improvements
  • Staff training and development
  • Updates to quality assurance procedures
  • Revision of policies and templates

Monitoring

  • Tracking complaint volumes and types
  • Measuring resolution timeframes
  • Assessing customer satisfaction with complaint handling
  • Reporting to senior management

10. Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR)

If we are unable to resolve your complaint through our internal process, you may wish to consider alternative dispute resolution options:

Mediation

We are willing to participate in mediation through an independent mediator. Mediation is typically:

  • Voluntary and confidential
  • Faster and less costly than litigation
  • Focused on finding mutually acceptable solutions

Professional Bodies

You may contact relevant professional bodies if you believe we have breached professional standards.

Small Claims Court

For disputes involving smaller amounts (currently up to £10,000 in England), you may pursue a claim through the small claims track of the county court.

Commercial Arbitration

For larger or more complex disputes, arbitration may be appropriate as specified in our service agreements.

11. Vexatious or Malicious Complaints

We reserve the right to refuse to consider complaints that are:

  • Made in bad faith or for malicious purposes
  • Repetitive without new evidence or information
  • Abusive, threatening, or harassing in nature
  • Clearly frivolous or without merit

We will inform you in writing if we determine a complaint falls into these categories, with reasons for our decision.

12. Data Protection

Personal information provided in complaints is processed in accordance with our Privacy Policy and UK GDPR requirements. We will:

  • Only use your information to investigate and respond to your complaint
  • Keep your information secure
  • Retain records only as long as necessary
  • Not share your information without your consent (except where legally required)

13. Policy Review

This policy is reviewed annually and updated as needed to reflect:

  • Changes in regulatory requirements
  • Best practices in complaint handling
  • Lessons learned from complaint trends
  • Feedback from clients and stakeholders
