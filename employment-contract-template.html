6. Remote Working Arrangement

6.1 Full Remote Working

The Company operates as a fully remote organization. Your normal place of work is your home address or such other remote location as may be agreed with the Company from time to time.

6.2 Home Working Requirements

When working remotely, you must:

  • Maintain a suitable, safe, and secure working environment;
  • Ensure you have reliable internet connectivity and appropriate equipment;
  • Be available during agreed working hours via email, phone, and video conferencing;
  • Comply with the Company's Remote Working Policy and IT Security policies;
  • Notify the Company of any changes to your remote working location;
  • Ensure your home insurance covers business use if applicable;
  • Maintain confidentiality and data security at all times.

6.3 Equipment

The Company will provide you with necessary equipment for remote working, including [specify: laptop, monitor, etc.]. All equipment remains the property of the Company and must be returned upon termination of employment.

6.4 Workspace Assessment

You are required to complete a home workspace self-assessment to ensure your working environment meets health and safety requirements. The Company reserves the right to conduct virtual or in-person workspace assessments as necessary.

6.5 Occasional Travel

While your role is fully remote, you may occasionally be required to travel for meetings, training, conferences, or Company events. Reasonable expenses incurred will be reimbursed in accordance with the Company's expenses policy.

7. Holiday Entitlement

7.1 Annual Leave

You are entitled to 28 days of paid annual leave per holiday year (pro-rated for part-year service), inclusive of the eight (8) public and bank holidays normally observed in England and Wales.

7.2 Holiday Year

The Company's holiday year runs from [e.g., 1 January to 31 December / 1 April to 31 March].

7.3 Booking and Approval

All holiday must be requested and approved in advance through the Company's holiday booking system. You must:

  • Give at least [e.g., two weeks'] notice for holiday requests;
  • Obtain approval from your line manager before booking any travel;
  • Not carry forward unused holiday except with express written permission;
  • Ensure adequate coverage for your responsibilities during absences.

7.4 Calculation on Termination

On termination of employment, you will be entitled to payment in lieu of accrued but untaken holiday, or required to repay the Company for any holiday taken in excess of your accrued entitlement, calculated on a pro-rata basis.

7.5 Company Right to Require Holiday

The Company reserves the right to require you to take holiday at specific times, including during the period between Christmas and New Year, or to preserve your entitlement at year-end, giving appropriate notice.

8. Sickness Absence

8.1 Notification

If you are unable to attend work due to sickness or injury, you must:

  • Notify your line manager as soon as possible on the first day of absence, and in any event before your normal start time;
  • Provide an indication of the expected duration of the absence;
  • Keep the Company informed of your progress and expected return date;
  • Comply with the Company's absence reporting procedures set out in the Employee Handbook.

8.2 Medical Certificates

For absences exceeding seven (7) consecutive calendar days, you must provide a doctor's Statement of Fitness for Work ("fit note"). The Company may also require medical certificates for shorter absences at its discretion.

8.3 Statutory Sick Pay

Subject to meeting eligibility criteria, you will be entitled to Statutory Sick Pay (SSP) in accordance with current legislation. [If applicable: The Company may provide occupational sick pay in accordance with the Company sick pay scheme, details of which are set out in the Employee Handbook.]

8.4 Medical Examinations

The Company may require you to undergo a medical examination by a doctor or occupational health specialist appointed by the Company at any time, and you consent to the disclosure of relevant medical information to the Company for this purpose.

9. Other Leave

9.1 Family-Related Leave

You are entitled to statutory family-related leave in accordance with UK employment law, including:

  • Maternity leave and pay;
  • Paternity leave and pay;
  • Adoption leave and pay;
  • Shared parental leave and pay;
  • Parental leave (unpaid);
  • Time off for dependants (unpaid).

Details of these entitlements and the procedures for requesting leave are set out in the Employee Handbook.

9.2 Other Statutory Leave

You are also entitled to other statutory leave rights as applicable, including bereavement leave in accordance with the Parental Bereavement Leave Regulations 2020.

10. Confidentiality

10.1 Confidential Information

During and after your employment, you must:

  • Keep confidential all Confidential Information of the Company, its clients, and business partners;
  • Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written authorization;
  • Not use Confidential Information for any purpose other than the proper performance of your duties;
  • Take all reasonable steps to prevent unauthorized disclosure or use.

10.2 Definition

"Confidential Information" includes, but is not limited to:

  • Trade secrets, know-how, and proprietary information;
  • Business plans, strategies, and financial information;
  • Client and customer information, including personal data;
  • Technical data, designs, software, and source code;
  • Marketing plans, pricing information, and supplier details;
  • Information about employees and contractors;
  • Any information marked as confidential or that you know or ought to know is confidential.

10.3 Exceptions

This obligation does not apply to information that:

  • Is in or becomes part of the public domain through no fault of yours;
  • You are required to disclose by law or court order;
  • Constitutes a protected disclosure under whistleblowing legislation (see Clause 19).

10.4 Return of Property

On termination of employment or at any time upon request, you must immediately return to the Company all property, documents, data, and Confidential Information in any form, including copies, and confirm in writing that you have complied with this obligation.

11. Intellectual Property

11.1 Assignment of Rights

All Intellectual Property Rights created, developed, or discovered by you during the course of your employment, whether during working hours or otherwise, and whether alone or with others, shall automatically vest in and belong to the Company absolutely.

11.2 Definition

"Intellectual Property Rights" means all patents, rights to inventions, copyright and related rights, trade marks, business names and domain names, rights in get-up, goodwill and the right to sue for passing off, rights in designs, database rights, rights to use and protect the confidentiality of confidential information (including know-how and trade secrets), and all other intellectual property rights, in each case whether registered or unregistered.

11.3 Disclosure and Assistance

You must:

  • Promptly disclose to the Company all inventions, developments, and works you create during your employment;
  • Execute all documents and do all acts necessary to vest such rights in the Company;
  • Provide all reasonable assistance to the Company in applying for, obtaining, and enforcing Intellectual Property Rights;
  • Not apply for any Intellectual Property Rights in your own name without prior written consent.

11.4 Moral Rights

You irrevocably waive all moral rights under the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 (and any equivalent rights in other jurisdictions) in relation to any works created during your employment.

11.5 Compensation

Your salary includes full compensation for all Intellectual Property Rights assigned to the Company. You acknowledge that you have no right to additional compensation for such assignments [except as may be required under the Patents Act 1977 for employee inventions of outstanding benefit to the Company].

12. Data Protection

12.1 Processing of Employee Data

The Company will process your personal data in accordance with its Privacy Policy and applicable data protection laws, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018. Details of how your data is processed are provided in the Employee Privacy Notice.

12.2 Employee Obligations

In the course of your employment, you may have access to personal data relating to clients, employees, or others. You must:

  • Process personal data only in accordance with the Company's instructions and data protection policies;
  • Maintain the confidentiality and security of personal data;
  • Report any actual or suspected data breaches immediately;
  • Complete all required data protection training;
  • Comply with all applicable data protection laws and regulations.

13. Post-Termination Restrictions

13.1 Non-Compete (Senior Positions Only)

[For senior/key positions only:] For a period of [e.g., 3/6] months following termination of employment, you shall not, without the prior written consent of the Company, directly or indirectly:

  • Be engaged, concerned, or interested in any business that competes with the business of the Company within [specify geographic area if relevant or "the territories in which you worked"];
  • Solicit or canvass for business from any client or prospective client with whom you had material dealings in the 12 months prior to termination.

13.2 Non-Solicitation of Clients

For a period of twelve (12) months following termination of employment, you shall not, without the prior written consent of the Company:

  • Solicit, canvass, or approach any person, firm, or company that was a client or active prospective client of the Company in the 12 months prior to termination with whom you had material dealings;
  • Accept business from such clients where solicited or approached.

13.3 Non-Solicitation of Employees

For a period of twelve (12) months following termination of employment, you shall not, without the prior written consent of the Company:

  • Solicit, entice away, or endeavor to employ or engage any employee, consultant, or contractor of the Company;
  • Assist others in doing so.

13.4 Reasonableness

You acknowledge that the restrictions in this Clause 13 are reasonable and necessary for the protection of the legitimate business interests of the Company, including its confidential information, client connections, and workforce stability. Each restriction is separate and severable.

14. Outside Interests and Conflicts

14.1 Devotion to Duties

During your employment, you shall devote your full working time, attention, and abilities to your duties and shall not, without prior written consent:

  • Undertake any other employment or engagement;
  • Hold any directorship or office in any other company or organization;
  • Engage in any business activity that competes with the Company's business;
  • Have any financial interest (other than holdings of not more than 3% in a publicly listed company) in any business that competes with the Company.

14.2 Disclosure of Conflicts

You must immediately disclose to the Company any situation that could give rise to a conflict of interest between your duties to the Company and your personal interests or duties to any third party.

15. Termination and Notice

15.1 Notice Periods

After successful completion of your probationary period, either party may terminate this contract by giving written notice as follows:

Notice by Employee: [Specify based on seniority, e.g.:]

  • One (1) month's written notice [for standard positions]
  • Three (3) months' written notice [for senior positions]

Notice by Company: [Specify based on seniority, e.g.:]

The Company reserves the right to terminate your employment with immediate effect by paying you salary in lieu of your notice period (or any unexpired portion thereof). Such payment will be subject to normal deductions for tax and National Insurance.

15.3 Garden Leave

During any notice period (whether given by you or the Company), the Company may require you to:

  • Not attend work or perform any duties;
  • Not contact clients, suppliers, or employees;
  • Remain available to assist with handover;
  • Continue to receive full salary and benefits.

You will remain employed during any garden leave period and must comply with all obligations under this contract.

15.4 Summary Dismissal

The Company may terminate your employment without notice or payment in lieu of notice if you commit an act of gross misconduct or are otherwise in fundamental breach of your obligations. Acts that may constitute gross misconduct are set out in the Employee Handbook.
