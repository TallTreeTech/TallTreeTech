Our Commitment

TallTree Technologies is committed to providing exceptional service to all our clients. We value feedback and take all complaints seriously. This policy outlines how we handle complaints fairly, efficiently, and professionally, ensuring every concern is addressed promptly and thoroughly.

Our Promise: We will listen, investigate, respond, and learn from every complaint to continuously improve our services.

Key Commitments:

  • 48-hour acknowledgment of all complaints
  • 14-day resolution target for standard complaints
  • Clear escalation process if dissatisfied with initial response
  • No detriment policy - complaints will not affect service or relationship
  • Continuous improvement - learning from all feedback

1. What is a Complaint?

A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction with our services, staff, processes, or business conduct that requires a formal response.

1.1 Examples of Complaints

  • Dissatisfaction with quality or timeliness of services
  • Concerns about billing, invoicing, or payment issues
  • Disagreement with technical decisions or recommendations
  • Issues with communication or responsiveness
  • Concerns about professional conduct or behaviour
  • Data protection or privacy concerns
  • Breach of contract or service level agreements
  • Discrimination or unfair treatment
  • Health and safety concerns

1.2 What is Not a Complaint?

The following are handled through normal business processes, not the formal complaints procedure:

  • First-time service requests: Standard requests for information or support
  • Queries: Questions about services, pricing, or contracts
  • Feedback: General suggestions or comments (though always welcome)
  • Formal disputes: Contractual disputes in active legal proceedings

3. Complaints Handling Process

3.1 Process Overview

Stage 1: Acknowledgment (Within 48 Hours)

We will acknowledge receipt of your complaint within 2 working days and provide:

  • Unique complaint reference number
  • Name and contact details of assigned Complaints Officer
  • Expected timeline for investigation and response
  • Confirmation of information received

Stage 2: Investigation (7-14 Days)

We will thoroughly investigate your complaint by:

  • Reviewing all relevant documentation and evidence
  • Interviewing involved staff members
  • Examining project files, correspondence, and records
  • Consulting with technical experts if required
  • Identifying root causes and contributing factors

Complex Complaints: If investigation requires longer than 14 days, we will inform you of the delay and provide regular updates.

Stage 3: Response (Within 14 Days)

We will provide a full written response including:

  • Summary of the complaint
  • Findings of the investigation
  • Explanation of what happened and why
  • Whether we uphold, partially uphold, or do not uphold the complaint
  • Actions taken or proposed to resolve the issue
  • Remedies or compensation offered (if applicable)
  • Steps taken to prevent recurrence
  • Right to escalate if dissatisfied

Stage 4: Resolution and Follow-up

After our response:

  • Implement agreed remedies or corrective actions
  • Follow up to ensure satisfaction with resolution
  • Update internal processes to prevent recurrence
  • Close complaint file with full documentation

4. Escalation Levels

4.1 Level 1: Complaints Officer

Initial Handling: All complaints are initially handled by the designated Complaints Officer

Authority: Can offer remedies, service credits, apologies, and process improvements

4.2 Level 2: Senior Management Review

When to Escalate:

  • You are dissatisfied with the Level 1 response
  • You believe the investigation was inadequate
  • New evidence has emerged
  • The complaint involves serious allegations

Reviewed by: Director or Senior Manager (independent of original investigation)

Process: Complete re-investigation with fresh perspective

Response Time: 14 working days from escalation request

To Escalate: Email complaints@talltreetech.co.uk with "ESCALATION REQUEST" in subject line

4.3 Level 3: Director Review (Final Internal Review)

When to Escalate:

  • Still dissatisfied after Level 2 review
  • Fundamental disagreement on facts or resolution
  • Request for final internal review before external escalation

Reviewed by: Company Director (highest internal authority)

Process: Comprehensive review of all previous investigations and responses

Response Time: 21 working days

Final Decision: Director's decision is final internal decision of the company

5. Investigation Procedures

5.1 Investigation Principles

All investigations follow these core principles:

Impartiality

Investigations are conducted by individuals with no direct involvement in the complaint matter. If a conflict of interest exists, an alternative investigator is appointed.

Thoroughness

All relevant evidence is gathered and considered, including documents, correspondence, witness statements, and technical records.

Confidentiality

Information is only shared on a need-to-know basis. Personal data is handled in accordance with UK GDPR and our Data Protection Policy.

Fairness

All parties have the opportunity to provide their account. Staff members are informed of allegations and can respond before conclusions are reached.

Timeliness

Investigations are completed as quickly as possible while ensuring thoroughness. Delays are communicated with explanation.

5.2 Investigation Steps

  1. Scope Definition: Clearly define what is being investigated
  2. Evidence Gathering: Collect all relevant documents, emails, records, and logs
  3. Witness Interviews: Speak with complainant, staff, and any witnesses
  4. Technical Review: Examine technical work, code, documentation if applicable
  5. Timeline Construction: Build accurate chronology of events
  6. Root Cause Analysis: Identify underlying causes, not just symptoms
  7. Findings: Determine what happened and whether standards were met
  8. Recommendations: Propose remedies and preventative actions
  9. Report: Document investigation in formal report
  10. Response: Communicate findings to complainant

5.3 Standard of Proof

Complaints are assessed on the "balance of probabilities" standard - whether it is more likely than not that the events occurred as described.

6. Remedies and Compensation

6.1 Types of Remedies

When a complaint is upheld or partially upheld, we may offer:

Remedy Type Description Examples
Apology Formal acknowledgment of error or failing Written apology from Director, in-person apology meeting
Explanation Clear explanation of what happened and why Detailed report, technical briefing
Service Credits Credit against future services 10-50% credit on next invoice, free hours
Refund Return of fees for substandard work Partial or full refund of specific deliverables
Remedial Work Correcting deficient work at no charge Bug fixes, rework, additional testing
Process Changes Improvements to prevent recurrence New quality checks, enhanced communication
Training Staff training to address skills gaps Technical training, customer service training
Policy Updates Changes to company policies or procedures Updated SLAs, revised escalation procedures
Financial Compensation Payment for losses or significant inconvenience Reimbursement of expenses, compensation for time lost

6.2 Compensation Guidelines

Minor Issues (Low Impact)

Examples: Delayed response, minor communication issues

Typical Remedy: Apology, explanation, process improvement

Moderate Issues (Medium Impact)

Examples: Missed deadlines, quality issues requiring rework

Typical Remedy: Apology + service credit (10-25%) OR remedial work at no charge

Significant Issues (High Impact)

Examples: Major service failures, data breaches, contractual breaches

Typical Remedy: Formal apology + significant service credit (25-50%) OR partial refund + remedial actions

Serious Issues (Critical Impact)

Examples: Gross negligence, professional misconduct, severe data breach

Typical Remedy: Director-level apology + substantial refund (50-100%) + financial compensation + comprehensive corrective actions

6.3 Accepting Remedies

Remedies are offered in good faith to resolve complaints. Accepting a remedy does not:

  • Prevent you from seeking further internal escalation
  • Constitute admission of legal liability beyond what is stated
  • Preclude alternative dispute resolution or legal action
Full and Final Settlement: If we offer financial compensation or significant refunds, we may request that you sign a settlement agreement confirming the complaint is fully resolved. This will be clearly stated in the offer.

9. Confidentiality and No Detriment Policy

9.1 Confidentiality

Complaints are handled with strict confidentiality:

  • Information is shared only with those directly involved in investigation or resolution
  • Anonymous complaints are kept anonymous
  • Complaint details are not disclosed to third parties without consent
  • Staff members are instructed to maintain confidentiality
Exceptions: We may be required to disclose complaint information:
  • If required by law or court order
  • To our insurers (professional indemnity claims)
  • To regulatory or professional bodies if serious misconduct is identified
  • With your explicit consent

9.2 No Detriment Policy

Our Guarantee: Making a complaint will NOT:

  • Affect the quality or delivery of services you receive
  • Impact your relationship with TallTree Technologies
  • Result in less favourable treatment
  • Affect future contract renewals or opportunities
  • Lead to retaliation or victimisation of any kind

We treat all clients fairly and professionally, regardless of whether they have made complaints.

9.3 Vexatious or Malicious Complaints

While we welcome all genuine complaints, we reserve the right to:

  • Not investigate complaints that are clearly vexatious, malicious, or frivolous
  • Close repeated complaints on the same issue after full investigation and response
  • Decline complaints submitted after unreasonable delay (beyond 12 months)
  • Limit contact if communication becomes abusive or threatening

We will always explain our reasoning if we decline to investigate a complaint.

10. Alternative Dispute Resolution

10.1 Mediation

If internal resolution is not successful, we may jointly agree to independent mediation:

What is Mediation?

Independent third-party mediator helps both parties reach mutually acceptable resolution.

Cost: Typically shared equally between parties

Timeframe: Usually resolved in 1-2 days

Organisations:

  • Centre for Effective Dispute Resolution (CEDR)
  • ADR Civil Mediation

10.2 Arbitration

For contractual disputes, parties may agree to binding arbitration:

What is Arbitration?

Independent arbitrator makes binding decision after hearing both sides.

Organisations:

  • Chartered Institute of Arbitrators
  • London Court of International Arbitration

10.3 Small Claims Court

For financial disputes under £10,000, you may pursue a claim through the Small Claims Court:

Small Claims Court (England & Wales)

Informal court process for smaller financial claims.

Limit: Up to £10,000

Information: Make a Court Claim for Money - GOV.UK
