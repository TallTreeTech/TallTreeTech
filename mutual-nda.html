Background

The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship (the "Purpose") and in connection with this Purpose, each Party may disclose to the other certain confidential and proprietary information. This Agreement sets out the terms upon which each Party will disclose Confidential Information (as defined below) to the other Party.

1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 Definitions

In this Agreement, the following terms shall have the following meanings:

  • "Confidential Information" means all information (whether written, oral, visual, electronic or in any other form) disclosed by or on behalf of one Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party") relating to the business, affairs, customers, clients, suppliers, plans, intentions, know-how, trade secrets, market opportunities, operations, processes, product information, software, designs, business models, financial information, technical data, prototypes, strategies, and any other information designated as confidential or which ought reasonably to be considered confidential given the nature of the information or the circumstances of disclosure.
  • "Purpose" means the evaluation and discussion of a potential business relationship between the Parties, including but not limited to consulting engagements, project collaborations, retainer arrangements, or fractional services.
  • "Representative" means in relation to a Party, its directors, officers, employees, agents, professional advisers, and contractors who have a legitimate need to know the Confidential Information for the Purpose.

1.2 Interpretation

References to clauses are to clauses of this Agreement. Headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation.

2. Confidentiality Obligations

2.1 Duty of Confidentiality

Each Party agrees that it shall:

  • Keep the Confidential Information of the other Party confidential and secret;
  • Not disclose or make available the Confidential Information to any person except as permitted under this Agreement;
  • Not use the Confidential Information for any purpose other than the Purpose;
  • Protect the Confidential Information using the same degree of care as it uses to protect its own confidential information of a similar nature, but in any event using no less than reasonable care;
  • Not copy, reproduce, or reduce to writing any Confidential Information except as strictly necessary for the Purpose.

2.2 Permitted Disclosures

The Receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information to its Representatives provided that:

  • Such Representatives have a genuine need to know the Confidential Information for the Purpose;
  • The Receiving Party informs such Representatives of the confidential nature of the information and ensures they are bound by obligations of confidentiality no less onerous than those contained in this Agreement;
  • The Receiving Party remains responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its Representatives.

3. Exceptions

3.1 Exclusions from Confidential Information

The obligations in Clause 2 shall not apply to information that:

  • Is or becomes publicly available other than through breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party;
  • Was lawfully in the possession of the Receiving Party prior to disclosure by the Disclosing Party, as evidenced by written records;
  • Is lawfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party without restriction and without breach of any obligation of confidence;
  • Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to or use of the Confidential Information, as evidenced by written records;
  • Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, court order, or other governmental or regulatory authority.

3.2 Required Disclosures

If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, or order of a court or regulatory authority to disclose any Confidential Information, it shall:

  • Notify the Disclosing Party promptly in writing of such requirement (unless prohibited by law);
  • Cooperate with the Disclosing Party in seeking protective measures;
  • Disclose only the minimum information necessary to comply with the legal requirement.

4. Protected Disclosures

4.1 Whistleblowing and Legal Rights

Nothing in this Agreement prevents or restricts either Party or any of their Representatives from:

  • Making a protected disclosure under the Public Interest Disclosure Act 1998 (whistleblowing);
  • Reporting suspected criminal conduct to the police, relevant regulatory authorities, or law enforcement agencies;
  • Disclosing information in compliance with the Victims and Prisoners Act 2024, including disclosures related to criminal conduct;
  • Reporting discrimination, harassment, or sexual harassment to appropriate authorities or regulators;
  • Cooperating with regulatory investigations or legal proceedings;
  • Disclosing information to their legal representatives for the purpose of obtaining legal advice;
  • Exercising any right or complying with any obligation under employment law or applicable regulations.

4.2 No Liability for Protected Disclosures

No Party shall have any liability under this Agreement for any disclosure made in accordance with Clause 4.1, and such disclosures shall not be considered a breach of this Agreement.

5. Term and Termination

5.1 Term

This Agreement shall commence on the date of last signature below and shall continue in force for a period of five (5) years from that date, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Clause.

5.2 Termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving thirty (30) days' written notice to the other Party.

5.3 Survival

Notwithstanding termination, the obligations of confidentiality under this Agreement shall survive and continue for a period of three (3) years from the date of termination or until such time as the Confidential Information ceases to be confidential in accordance with Clause 3.1, whichever occurs first.

5.4 End of Engagement

This Agreement shall automatically terminate upon the completion or cessation of any business engagement between the Parties, subject to the survival provisions in Clause 5.3.

6. Return and Destruction of Information

Upon termination of this Agreement or at any time upon written request by the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall (at the Disclosing Party's election):

  • Return all documents and materials containing Confidential Information to the Disclosing Party; or
  • Destroy all documents and materials containing Confidential Information and certify such destruction in writing;
  • Provided that the Receiving Party may retain one copy of the Confidential Information solely for the purpose of demonstrating compliance with this Agreement or as required by applicable law, subject to the continuing obligations of confidentiality.

7. No Rights Granted

7.1 No License

Nothing in this Agreement grants any license, right, title, or interest in any intellectual property rights of either Party. All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party.

7.2 No Obligation to Proceed

This Agreement does not create any obligation on either Party to:

  • Disclose any particular information;
  • Continue discussions or negotiations;
  • Enter into any further agreement or business relationship;
  • Refrain from entering into discussions or agreements with third parties.

8. No Warranty

All Confidential Information is provided "as is" without warranty of any kind, express or implied. Neither Party makes any representation or warranty as to the accuracy or completeness of any Confidential Information disclosed.

9. Remedies

9.1 Equitable Relief

The Parties acknowledge that damages may not be an adequate remedy for breach of this Agreement and that the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief and/or specific performance in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

9.2 No Waiver

No failure or delay by either Party in exercising any right or remedy under this Agreement shall operate as a waiver of that right or remedy.

10. General Provisions

10.1 Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties with respect to the subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, and arrangements (whether oral or written) relating to confidentiality.

10.2 Amendment

No amendment or variation of this Agreement shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by authorised representatives of both Parties.

10.3 Severability

If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect. The Parties shall negotiate in good faith to replace any invalid or unenforceable provision with a valid and enforceable provision that achieves the original intent.

10.4 Assignment

Neither Party may assign or transfer any rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, except that either Party may assign this Agreement to a successor in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all of its assets.
