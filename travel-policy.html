Business Travel and Expenses Policy

TallTree® Technologies

Effective Date: [DD/MM/YYYY] | Version 1.0

Policy Summary: This policy establishes guidelines for business travel and expense reimbursement for employees and contractors of TallTree Technologies. It ensures consistent, fair, and cost-effective travel practices while maintaining high service standards for our clients.

1. Policy Scope and Purpose

1.1 Purpose

This policy aims to:

  • Provide clear guidelines for planning and booking business travel
  • Ensure cost-effective travel arrangements that balance economy with employee comfort and productivity
  • Establish consistent expense reimbursement procedures
  • Ensure compliance with HMRC regulations and tax requirements
  • Support environmental sustainability in business travel decisions

1.2 Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All TallTree Technologies employees
  • Contractors and consultants when travel is billed to clients or reimbursed by TallTree
  • All travel undertaken for business purposes, including client visits, conferences, training, and company events

1.3 Definitions

  • Business Travel: Any travel outside of the normal commute required to fulfill job duties
  • Permanent Workplace: Your regular office or primary work location
  • Temporary Workplace: A location where you work for a limited duration (e.g., client site)
  • Reasonable Expenses: Necessary, appropriate, and properly documented costs incurred for business purposes

2. Pre-Approval Requirements

2.1 Approval Authority

Travel Type Estimated Cost Required Approval
UK Day Travel Up to £200 Line Manager
UK Overnight Travel Up to £500 Line Manager
International Travel (Europe) Up to £1,500 Director
International Travel (Non-Europe) Any amount Director + Finance
Any Travel Over £1,500 Director + Finance

2.2 Advance Notice Requirements

  • UK Travel: Request approval at least 5 working days in advance
  • European Travel: Request approval at least 10 working days in advance
  • International (Non-Europe): Request approval at least 15 working days in advance
Important: Failure to obtain pre-approval may result in expenses being denied for reimbursement. In exceptional circumstances where advance approval is not possible, contact your line manager immediately.

2.3 Client-Billable Travel

When travel costs will be billed to a client:

  • Verify that the Statement of Work or contract permits expense reimbursement
  • Confirm any client-specific travel policies or restrictions
  • Ensure proper documentation for client billing purposes
  • Mark expenses clearly as "Client Billable - [Client Name]" when submitting

3. Booking Procedures

3.1 General Booking Principles

  • Book as far in advance as possible to secure the best rates
  • Choose the most economical option that meets business needs
  • Consider total journey time, productivity, and employee wellbeing
  • Prioritize direct routes and minimize connection times where practical
  • Consider environmental impact and choose sustainable options when available

3.2 Preferred Booking Methods

  1. Company Travel Portal: [Portal name/URL if applicable] - Use for best rates and automatic approval routing
  2. Direct Booking: Book directly with suppliers (airlines, hotels, rail companies) and submit for reimbursement
  3. Corporate Card: Use company credit card where issued (see Section 10)

4. Transportation

4.1 Rail Travel

Rail Travel Standards

Journey Duration Permitted Class Notes
Under 2 hours Standard Class Required
2-4 hours Standard Class First Class with prior approval if significant work required
Over 4 hours First Class Permitted

Additional Guidelines:

  • Book advance tickets where possible for significant savings
  • Consider railcards if frequent travel (16-25, 26-30, Senior, Network Railcard)
  • London travel: Use Oyster/contactless for best rates within zones
  • Retain all tickets and receipts, even for contactless payments

4.2 Air Travel

Air Travel Standards

Flight Duration Permitted Class Notes
Under 5 hours Economy Required
5-8 hours Premium Economy With Director approval
Over 8 hours Business Class With Director approval

Additional Guidelines:

  • Book at least 14 days in advance for best economy fares
  • Choose direct flights where practical, even if slightly more expensive
  • Consider alternative airports if significant savings (factor in transfer costs and time)
  • Checked baggage: One bag included where necessary; additional bags require justification
  • Seat selection: Standard seat included; premium seat selection requires approval
  • Travel insurance: Included in company policy; do not purchase separate insurance

4.3 Personal Vehicle Use

Mileage Reimbursement: £0.45 per mile for the first 10,000 miles in a tax year, £0.25 per mile thereafter (in accordance with HMRC approved rates)

Conditions for Personal Vehicle Use:

  • Must hold valid UK driving license and insurance (business use cover required)
  • Vehicle must be roadworthy and meet MOT requirements
  • Only use personal vehicle when more economical or practical than public transport
  • Compare costs with rail/air travel before choosing to drive
  • Mileage calculated from permanent workplace or home (whichever is closer) to destination

Parking and Tolls: Reimbursable with receipts. Use economy parking options where available.

Passenger Miles: Additional 5p per mile per passenger if carrying colleagues on business travel

4.4 Rental Vehicles

  • Authorization: Required when public transport is impractical or more expensive
  • Vehicle Class: Economy or compact cars are standard; larger vehicles require justification
  • Insurance: Decline collision damage waiver (CDW) if covered by company or personal insurance; check before declining
  • Fuel Policy: Return vehicle with same fuel level as pickup to avoid inflated refueling charges
  • Additional Drivers: Add as required if cost is reasonable and multiple people will drive

4.5 Taxis and Ride-Sharing

When Permitted:

  • Public transport is not available or practical
  • Travel to/from airports or stations with luggage
  • Late evening or early morning travel when public transport is limited
  • When traveling as a group and total cost is comparable to individual public transport
  • Time sensitivity justifies the additional cost

Standards:

  • Use licensed taxis or reputable ride-sharing services (Uber, Bolt, etc.)
  • Obtain itemized receipts for all journeys
  • Choose standard vehicles; premium/luxury vehicles not reimbursable without exceptional justification

5. Accommodation Standards

5.1 Hotel Selection Criteria

Choose hotels that are:

  • Conveniently located to the work site or meeting location
  • Safe and in reputable areas
  • Mid-range business hotels (3-star or equivalent)
  • Reasonably priced for the location

5.2 Nightly Accommodation Rates

Maximum Nightly Rates (Room + Taxes)

Location Maximum Rate
London (Central) £180 per night
London (Outer), Major UK Cities £120 per night
Other UK Locations £90 per night
European Capitals/Major Cities £150 per night
Other European Locations £110 per night
International (Outside Europe) Case by case - pre-approval required
Note: These are maximum rates, not targets. Always seek the most economical option that meets business needs. Rates exceeding these limits require advance approval with justification.

5.3 Hotel Amenities and Extras

Included/Reimbursable:

  • Standard room accommodation and taxes
  • WiFi access (if charged separately)
  • Breakfast (if not included in room rate, see meal allowances)

Not Reimbursable:

  • Room upgrades beyond standard business room
  • In-room movies, entertainment, or mini-bar
  • Spa services, gym fees (unless required for client meetings)
  • Personal laundry (except on trips exceeding 5 consecutive nights)

5.4 Alternative Accommodation

  • Serviced Apartments: Permitted for stays of 5+ nights where cost-effective
  • Airbnb/Holiday Rentals: Permitted if professional, safe, and more economical than hotels
  • Extended Stays: Negotiate weekly rates for long-term assignments

Location Daily Maximum (All Meals)

London £50 per day

Meal Allowance Guidelines:
  • Allowances cover reasonable meal costs; always choose mid-range restaurants
  • If breakfast is included in hotel rate, deduct £8 from daily allowance
  • Alcohol: Up to one alcoholic drink with dinner is acceptable; excessive alcohol is not reimbursable
  • Receipts required for all meals over £10
  • Meals included in conference/event fees are not additionally reimbursable

6.3 Client Meals and Entertainment

When entertaining clients or prospects:

  • Requires advance approval from Director
  • Choose appropriate venue for the client relationship and business context
  • Reasonable alcohol is acceptable; excessive consumption is not
  • Itemized receipts required with names of attendees and business purpose
  • Tip: 10-15% is reasonable for good service

8. Expense Claims Process

8.1 Submitting Expense Claims

Timeline:

  • Submit expense claims within 30 days of incurring the expense
  • Claims submitted after 90 days may not be reimbursed
  • Submit monthly for regular travelers

Submission Process:

  1. Complete expense claim form via [expense system/portal]
  2. Attach all original receipts or scanned copies (photos acceptable if legible)
  3. Provide clear business purpose for each expense
  4. Indicate if expense is client-billable
  5. Submit to line manager for approval
  6. Finance processes approved claims within 10 working days

8.2 Receipt Requirements

Expense Type Receipt Requirement
Under £10 Receipt preferred but not mandatory with explanation
£10 - £25 Receipt required
Over £25 Itemized receipt required (credit card slip alone not sufficient)
All Hotels Itemized hotel bill required regardless of amount
All Flights/Rail E-ticket/booking confirmation and receipt/proof of payment required
Mileage Mileage log with dates, destinations, business purpose, and miles

8.3 Required Information

Every expense claim must include:

  • Date of expense
  • Amount in original currency (converted to GBP if foreign)
  • Vendor/supplier name
  • Clear business purpose
  • Client name (if billable)
  • Project code (if applicable)
  • VAT amount (if applicable and shown on receipt)

8.4 Foreign Currency Expenses

  • Submit expenses in the currency paid
  • System will convert using HMRC rates for the transaction date
  • Credit card exchange rates may differ from reimbursement rates
  • Foreign transaction fees from personal credit cards are not reimbursable (use company card to avoid)

10. Company Credit Card Policy

10.1 Eligibility

Company credit cards may be issued to:

  • Employees who travel frequently for business (6+ trips per year)
  • Senior staff and directors
  • Project managers managing client-billable expenses

10.2 Permitted Use

Approved:

  • All business travel expenses covered by this policy
  • Client-billable project expenses
  • Business-related purchases with proper authorization

Prohibited:

  • Personal expenses of any kind
  • Cash advances (except with pre-approval for international travel)
  • Expenses that circumvent standard procurement processes

10.3 Reconciliation Requirements

  • Review monthly statement within 5 days of receipt
  • Submit expense report matching all charges within 10 days
  • Provide receipts for all transactions over £25
  • Query any unrecognized transactions immediately
  • Failure to reconcile on time may result in card suspension

10.4 Lost or Stolen Cards

  1. Report to card issuer immediately (24/7 helpline on back of card)
  2. Notify Finance Director within 24 hours
  3. Complete incident report
  4. New card will be issued within 3-5 working days

11. Sustainability Considerations

TallTree's Commitment to Sustainable Travel

We are committed to reducing the environmental impact of our business travel. Employees are encouraged to:

11.1 Travel Mode Selection

  • Virtual First: Consider video conferencing as a first option; travel only when in-person presence adds significant value
  • Rail Over Air: For UK and near-European destinations under 4 hours by rail, choose train over plane
  • Public Transport: Use public transport where practical instead of taxis or rental cars
  • Direct Flights: Choose direct flights to reduce emissions from takeoff/landing

11.2 Accommodation

  • Choose hotels with environmental certifications where available (Green Key, EarthCheck, etc.)
  • Reuse towels and linens to reduce water consumption
  • Decline daily cleaning service for multi-night stays

11.3 Carbon Offsetting

TallTree participates in carbon offset programs for business travel:

  • Voluntary carbon offset contribution added to flight bookings where available
  • Annual carbon footprint assessment and offset purchase for company travel
  • Reporting on travel emissions reduction goals

12. Health and Safety

12.1 Pre-Travel Health Checks

For international travel:

  • Check Foreign Office travel advice for destination
  • Verify visa and passport requirements (passport must be valid for 6+ months)
  • Confirm required vaccinations and medications (consult travel clinic 6-8 weeks in advance)
  • Register trip with company (for emergency contact purposes)

12.2 During Travel

  • Keep copy of passport and important documents separate from originals
  • Share itinerary with line manager and emergency contact
  • Maintain regular communication, especially in high-risk locations
  • Follow local health and safety protocols
  • Use hotel safes for valuables and sensitive documents

12.3 Travel Risk Assessment

Travel to locations with Foreign Office warnings or elevated risk requires:

  • Formal risk assessment by line manager and Director
  • Written approval from Director
  • Enhanced security briefing and measures
  • Daily check-in requirements

12.4 Emergency Situations

In case of emergency while traveling:

  1. Contact local emergency services (999 in UK, 112 in EU)
  2. Contact travel insurance emergency line: [Number]
  3. Notify TallTree emergency contact: [Number]
  4. Contact British Embassy/Consulate if abroad

13. Personal Travel Extensions

13.1 Combining Business and Personal Travel

Employees may extend business trips for personal reasons with approval, subject to:

  • No additional cost to the company
  • No disruption to business objectives or work commitments
  • Personal travel days are taken as annual leave
  • Business and personal expenses are clearly separated

13.2 Cost Allocation

Expense Reimbursement Policy
Flights Company pays equivalent of direct round-trip flight for business dates only. Employee pays any difference.
Accommodation Company pays for business nights only. Personal nights are employee's responsibility.
Meals Allowances apply to business days only.
Ground Transport Reimbursable for business portion only.

13.3 Tax Implications

Important Tax Notice: Personal travel extensions may create taxable benefits. Consult Finance before booking combined business/personal trips to understand potential tax implications.

14. Non-Compliance and Violations

14.1 Policy Violations

Violations of this travel policy may result in:

  • Denial of expense reimbursement
  • Requirement to personally pay for non-compliant expenses
  • Suspension of company credit card privileges
  • Disciplinary action up to and including termination

14.2 Examples of Serious Violations

  • Submitting fraudulent or falsified expense claims
  • Using company credit card for personal expenses
  • Claiming reimbursement for expenses already paid by company or client
  • Deliberately circumventing approval processes
  • Repeated failure to comply with policy after warnings

15. Exceptions and Special Circumstances

15.1 Requesting Exceptions

Exceptions to this policy may be granted for:

  • Disability accommodations or medical requirements
  • Unique business circumstances requiring upgraded travel
  • Locations where standard policy limits are unrealistic
  • Safety or security concerns

Exception Request Process:

  1. Submit written request to line manager explaining circumstances
  2. Provide justification and any supporting documentation
  3. Obtain Director approval before booking
  4. Document approval with expense claim submission
