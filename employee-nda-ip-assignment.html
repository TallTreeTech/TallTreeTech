Recitals

WHEREAS, the Employee is employed by the Company in the position of [Job Title] and will have access to Confidential Information and may create Intellectual Property in the course of employment;

WHEREAS, the Company has a legitimate business interest in protecting its Confidential Information, trade secrets, and Intellectual Property;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the employment relationship and the mutual covenants contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. Definitions

1.1 Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means all confidential, proprietary, or non-public information of the Company, its clients, suppliers, or business partners, in any form or medium, including but not limited to:

  • Technical Information: Inventions, discoveries, innovations, improvements, designs, specifications, formulas, methods, processes, techniques, algorithms, software, source code, object code, data structures, databases, systems architecture, technical documentation, research and development information;
  • Business Information: Business plans, strategies, methods, practices, financial information, budgets, forecasts, costs, pricing, profit margins, sales information, marketing plans, market research, business opportunities;
  • Client and Customer Information: Client lists, customer information, contact details, requirements, preferences, purchasing history, contracts, proposals, negotiations;
  • Employee and Personnel Information: Employee records, salary information, performance data, organizational structure;
  • Third Party Information: Information received from clients, partners, or suppliers under obligations of confidence;
  • Any Other Information: That is marked as confidential, designated as confidential, or that the Employee knows or ought reasonably to know is confidential or proprietary to the Company.

1.2 Intellectual Property

"Intellectual Property" or "IP" means all intellectual property and proprietary rights, including but not limited to:

  • Patents and patent applications;
  • Inventions (whether patentable or not);
  • Trade secrets, know-how, and confidential information;
  • Copyright and related rights (including software, documentation, designs, graphics);
  • Trade marks, service marks, trade names, brand names, logos;
  • Database rights and rights in data and information;
  • Design rights (registered and unregistered);
  • Domain names and URLs;
  • Rights in get-up and trade dress;
  • All other intellectual and industrial property rights of any kind;

in each case, whether registered or unregistered, and including all applications for registration and renewals or extensions of such rights, and all similar or equivalent rights or forms of protection in any part of the world.

1.3 Work Product

"Work Product" means all works, inventions, discoveries, developments, improvements, innovations, designs, writings, software, data, materials, and other intellectual property of any kind created, developed, conceived, reduced to practice, or made by the Employee (whether alone or jointly with others) during the period of employment:

  • During working hours or using Company resources, equipment, or facilities;
  • In connection with or relating to the Employee's work for the Company;
  • That result from or are suggested by work performed for the Company;
  • That relate to the Company's actual or anticipated business, research, or development.

2. Confidentiality Obligations

2.1 Non-Disclosure During Employment

During the period of employment, the Employee shall:

  • Keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential and not disclose it to any third party;
  • Use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of performing duties as an employee of the Company;
  • Not use Confidential Information for the Employee's own benefit or the benefit of any third party;
  • Take all reasonable precautions to prevent unauthorized disclosure or use of Confidential Information;
  • Comply with all Company policies and procedures relating to information security and data protection;
  • Immediately report any actual, suspected, or threatened breach of confidentiality to the Company.

2.2 Post-Employment Confidentiality

The Employee's obligations of confidentiality shall continue indefinitely after termination of employment. Following termination, the Employee shall:

  • Continue to keep all Confidential Information strictly confidential;
  • Not disclose or use Confidential Information for any purpose;
  • Not exploit Confidential Information for commercial advantage or competitive purposes;
  • Return or destroy all Confidential Information as required by Clause 7.

2.3 Permitted Disclosures

The Employee may disclose Confidential Information only:

  • To the extent required to perform the Employee's duties, and only to persons who have a legitimate need to know and who are bound by confidentiality obligations;
  • With the prior written consent of the Company;
  • To the extent required by law, court order, or regulatory authority (provided the Employee gives the Company prompt written notice of such requirement to allow the Company to seek protective measures);
  • As a protected disclosure under whistleblowing legislation (see Clause 9).

2.4 Exclusions from Confidential Information

Information shall not be considered Confidential Information if it:

  • Is or becomes publicly available through no fault or breach of duty by the Employee;
  • Was already known to the Employee prior to employment without any obligation of confidentiality;
  • Is independently developed by the Employee after termination of employment without reference to or use of the Company's Confidential Information;
  • Is rightfully received by the Employee from a third party without breach of any confidentiality obligation.

2.5 Standard of Care

The Employee shall protect Confidential Information using at least the same degree of care as the Employee uses to protect their own confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care.

3. Intellectual Property Assignment

3.1 Automatic Assignment

The Employee hereby assigns, transfers, and conveys to the Company, with full title guarantee and free from all encumbrances, all right, title, and interest (including all Intellectual Property rights) in and to all Work Product.

This assignment operates as:

  • An immediate assignment of existing rights in Work Product already created;
  • An assignment of future rights in Work Product to be created during employment;
  • A present assignment of future copyright under section 91 of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988.

3.2 Scope of Assignment

The assignment includes all rights to:

  • Apply for, prosecute, obtain, and maintain Intellectual Property protection worldwide;
  • Use, make, sell, license, modify, and create derivative works;
  • Enforce rights and bring proceedings against infringers;
  • Assign, transfer, or license the rights to third parties;
  • Exploit the Work Product in any manner whatsoever.

3.3 Works Made for Hire

To the extent permitted by law, all Work Product shall be deemed "works made for hire" as defined in applicable copyright law, with the Company being deemed the author and owner.

3.4 Compensation

The Employee acknowledges and agrees that:

  • The Employee's salary fully compensates for all Work Product and IP assignments;
  • The Employee has no right to additional compensation for assignments under this Agreement;
  • Subject to the Patents Act 1977, the Employee shall not be entitled to compensation for employee inventions unless an invention is of outstanding benefit to the Company.

4. Employee Obligations Regarding IP

4.1 Disclosure of Inventions

The Employee shall promptly and fully disclose to the Company in writing:

  • All Work Product created during employment;
  • All inventions, discoveries, improvements, or innovations made during employment;
  • All software, documentation, designs, or other works created;
  • Sufficient detail to enable the Company to understand and evaluate the Work Product.

4.2 Assistance and Cooperation

The Employee shall, both during and after employment:

  • Execute all documents reasonably necessary to perfect the Company's ownership of Work Product;
  • Provide all reasonable assistance to the Company in applying for, obtaining, maintaining, and enforcing Intellectual Property rights;
  • Give evidence and testimony in any proceedings relating to Intellectual Property;
  • Cooperate in any IP-related matters at the Company's request;
  • Not contest or challenge the Company's ownership of Work Product or Intellectual Property.

4.3 Power of Attorney

The Employee hereby irrevocably appoints the Company as the Employee's attorney to execute documents and take actions on the Employee's behalf to perfect ownership of Work Product or Intellectual Property if the Employee is unavailable, unwilling, or unable to do so. This power of attorney is coupled with an interest and shall survive termination of employment.

4.4 Reimbursement of Expenses

The Company shall reimburse the Employee for all reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred by the Employee in providing assistance after termination of employment, subject to prior approval and provision of receipts.

5. Prior Inventions and Third-Party Rights

5.1 Declaration of Prior Inventions

The Employee has disclosed to the Company in Schedule 1 attached hereto all inventions, discoveries, writings, software, or other intellectual property created prior to employment that:

  • The Employee wishes to exclude from the scope of this Agreement;
  • The Employee has rights in and wishes to retain ownership of;
  • May be relevant to the Company's business or the Employee's work.

If Schedule 1 is blank or states "None", the Employee represents that there are no such Prior Inventions to disclose.

5.2 Exclusion of Prior Inventions

Prior Inventions properly disclosed in Schedule 1 are excluded from the assignment in Clause 3. However, if any Work Product incorporates, is based upon, or cannot be separated from Prior Inventions, the Employee hereby grants the Company a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive license to use, modify, and exploit such Prior Inventions as incorporated in the Work Product.

5.3 Third-Party Obligations

The Employee represents and warrants that:

  • The Employee is not bound by any agreement or obligation that would prevent performance of duties or assignment of Work Product;
  • The Employee will not use or disclose any confidential information or intellectual property of any former employer or third party;
  • The Employee will not bring to the Company or use in the Company's business any materials or documents of a former employer without proper authorization;
  • To the best of the Employee's knowledge, performance of duties will not infringe any third-party intellectual property rights.

5.4 Notification of Conflicts

The Employee shall immediately notify the Company if the Employee becomes aware of any conflict between obligations to the Company and any third-party obligations, or any claim that Work Product infringes third-party rights.

6. Moral Rights Waiver

6.1 Waiver of Moral Rights

The Employee irrevocably and unconditionally waives and agrees not to assert any and all moral rights (including rights of paternity, integrity, disclosure, and withdrawal) under the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988 and any equivalent rights under the laws of any jurisdiction worldwide in relation to all Work Product and any works created during employment.

6.2 Consent to Modifications

The Employee consents to any modification, adaptation, deletion from, or addition to any Work Product, and to the use of Work Product in any context, even if such use or modification might otherwise infringe moral rights.

6.3 Attribution

The Company may, but is not obligated to, credit or attribute authorship to the Employee. The Employee acknowledges that the Company may use Work Product without attribution.

7. Return of Property and Information

7.1 Company Property

All documents, materials, equipment, data, and information (in any form or medium) relating to the Company's business, including all Confidential Information, are and remain the sole property of the Company.

7.2 Return Upon Termination

Upon termination of employment, or at any time upon request by the Company, the Employee shall immediately return to the Company:

  • All Company property, including equipment, mobile phones, computers, access cards, keys;
  • All documents, files, records, data, and materials (whether in hard copy or electronic form);
  • All Confidential Information in any form or medium;
  • All copies, extracts, or reproductions of any of the above;
  • All property belonging to clients or third parties in the Employee's possession.

7.3 Deletion and Destruction

The Employee shall:

  • Permanently delete all Confidential Information from personal devices, cloud storage, email accounts, and any other locations;
  • Confirm deletion in writing to the Company;
  • Not retain any copies, extracts, or reproductions in any form;
  • Provide certification of compliance upon request.

7.4 Right to Inspect

The Company reserves the right to inspect the Employee's personal devices and storage media to verify compliance with this Clause 7, subject to reasonable notice and applicable privacy laws.

8. Data Protection and Privacy

8.1 Processing of Personal Data

In the course of employment, the Employee may have access to personal data relating to clients, employees, suppliers, or others. The Employee acknowledges that such personal data is Confidential Information and agrees to:

  • Process personal data only in accordance with the Company's instructions and data protection policies;
  • Comply with all applicable data protection laws, including the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018;
  • Maintain the confidentiality and security of personal data;
  • Not access, use, or disclose personal data except as necessary for employment duties;
  • Report any actual or suspected personal data breach immediately;
  • Complete all required data protection training.

8.2 Employee's Personal Data

The Employee acknowledges that the Company will process the Employee's personal data in accordance with its Privacy Policy and Employee Privacy Notice. By signing this Agreement, the Employee consents to such processing as necessary for employment purposes.

9. Whistleblowing and Protected Disclosures

9.1 Protected Disclosures

Nothing in this Agreement shall prevent the Employee from:

  • Making a protected disclosure under the Public Interest Disclosure Act 1998 (whistleblowing);
  • Reporting concerns about wrongdoing to appropriate regulatory authorities;
  • Cooperating with law enforcement or regulatory investigations;
  • Exercising statutory rights under employment law.

9.2 No Waiver of Rights

The Employee's confidentiality obligations do not prevent disclosure of information:

  • To the extent required by law or court order;
  • To legal advisors for the purpose of obtaining legal advice;
  • That constitutes a qualifying disclosure under whistleblowing legislation;
  • That is necessary to defend against legal proceedings brought by the Company.

9.3 Internal Reporting

The Employee is encouraged to report concerns about wrongdoing internally through the Company's Whistleblowing Policy before making external disclosures, although this is not a requirement for protection under whistleblowing law.

10. Reasonableness and Enforceability

10.1 Acknowledgment of Reasonableness

The Employee acknowledges and agrees that:

  • The restrictions and obligations in this Agreement are reasonable and necessary to protect the Company's legitimate business interests;
  • The Employee has received adequate consideration for these obligations in the form of employment and salary;
  • The Employee has had the opportunity to seek independent legal advice before signing;
  • The restrictions do not prevent the Employee from earning a livelihood.

10.2 Severability

Each provision of this Agreement is separate and severable. If any provision is found to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect. Any invalid provision shall be deemed modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it valid and enforceable.

10.3 Blue Pencil

If any restriction in this Agreement is found to be too wide in scope, duration, or application, it shall be deemed modified to the extent necessary to make it enforceable while preserving the Company's legitimate interests to the maximum extent possible.

11. Remedies and Enforcement

11.1 Injunctive Relief

The Employee acknowledges that:

  • Breach of this Agreement would cause irreparable harm to the Company;
  • Monetary damages would be an inadequate remedy;
  • The Company shall be entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable relief without the need to post a bond;
  • Such equitable remedies are in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

11.2 Survival of Obligations

The Employee's obligations under this Agreement shall survive termination of employment indefinitely, except as otherwise expressly provided.

11.3 No Waiver

No failure or delay by the Company in exercising any right shall constitute a waiver of that right. No waiver shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by an authorized representative of the Company.

12. General Provisions

12.1 Entire Agreement

This Agreement, together with the Employee's employment contract and any documents expressly referred to herein, constitutes the entire agreement relating to confidentiality and intellectual property and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, and arrangements.

12.2 Relationship to Employment Contract

This Agreement is supplemental to and does not replace the Employee's employment contract. In the event of any conflict, the provision most favorable to the Company shall prevail.

12.3 Amendments

No amendment to this Agreement shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by both parties.

12.4 Assignment

The Company may assign this Agreement to any successor or assignee. The Employee may not assign this Agreement. This Agreement shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the parties and their respective successors and permitted assigns.

12.5 Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales. The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.

12.6 Notices

Any notice under this Agreement shall be given in accordance with the notice provisions in the Employee's employment contract.

Key Obligations Summary

  • Confidentiality: Strict confidentiality during and after employment
  • IP Assignment: All work-related IP automatically assigned to Company
  • Prior Inventions: Must declare any prior IP to retain ownership
  • Disclosure: Must promptly disclose all inventions and work product
  • Moral Rights: Waived for all work created during employment
  • Return of Property: All Company property and information must be returned
  • Data Protection: Must comply with data protection laws and policies
  • Whistleblowing: Protected disclosures are not restricted
